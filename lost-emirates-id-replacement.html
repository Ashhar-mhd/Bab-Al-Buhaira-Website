Lost Emirates ID: how to replace it

Lost your Emirates ID, had it stolen, or is the card damaged? You need to apply for a replacement card. Here is what to do, and we can apply for you.

Ask on WhatsApp View Emirates ID service

What to do if your Emirates ID is lost

  1. Get a lost item certificate from the police. For a lost or stolen card, we first get a lost item certificate (police report) through Sharjah Police or Dubai Police. It is not mandatory, but we recommend it.
  2. Apply for a replacement card with ICP. This is the same request for a lost, stolen or damaged card.
  3. Collect the new card when it is ready. We tell you how and when for your case.

How to apply

The replacement is applied for with ICP (the Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs and Port Security), online or through an accredited typing centre. We are a typing centre in Al Majaz 2, Sharjah. Send us your details on WhatsApp and we submit the application for you.

Need it urgently?

ICP has an urgent service that issues the card within 24 hours, and it covers lost and damaged cards. For this you visit an ICP customer happiness centre. For expatriate residents, the urgent service is only for replacement cards, not new ones.

Card damaged, not lost?

A damaged card is replaced the same way, and no police report is needed. Keep the damaged card with you, in case it is asked for.

What to send us

  • Passport copy with your UAE visa
  • Your Emirates ID number, or a copy of the old card if you have one
  • A recent photo with a white background, face clearly visible

ICP fees depend on the card. We tell you the exact total before you pay, with the government fee and our service charge listed separately.

Questions

Lost Emirates ID: common questions

I lost my Emirates ID. What should I do first?

We usually first get a lost item certificate (police report) through Sharjah Police or Dubai Police. It is not mandatory but recommended. Then we apply for the replacement card with ICP for you.

Do I need a police report for a damaged Emirates ID?

No. A police report is only useful when the card is lost or stolen. A damaged card is replaced with ICP directly.

Can I get a replacement Emirates ID urgently?

ICP has an urgent service that issues the card within 24 hours for lost or damaged cards. You need to visit an ICP customer happiness centre for it.

What do I need to replace a lost Emirates ID?

Usually a passport copy with your UAE visa, your Emirates ID number and a recent photo with a white background. We confirm the list for your case.
