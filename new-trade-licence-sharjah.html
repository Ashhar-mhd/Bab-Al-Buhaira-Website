Getting a new trade licence in Sharjah

A mainland business in Sharjah needs a licence from the Sharjah Economic Development Department (SEDD). Here are SEDD's steps, the licence types and the documents, and how we can help.

Licence types SEDD lists

  • Commercial: buying, selling, import and export
  • Professional: services and consulting, such as management, IT, design or training. Some need professional certificates
  • Industrial: manufacturing and production, in an industrial area with extra approvals
  • E-commerce: online and social media sales
  • Eitimad: home business for UAE citizens

SEDD says foreigners can own 100% of most mainland businesses since 2021, though some activities are kept for citizens. A sole proprietorship for commercial or industrial activity is only for UAE or GCC nationals.

The steps

  1. Trade name: choose a name and keep 2 or 3 alternatives. Names that copy a registered name, use a country or government name, or mislead are refused
  2. Initial approval for the name and activity
  3. Lease contract attested by Sharjah City Municipality, after the SEWA electricity deposit. See tenancy contract attestation
  4. Technical evaluation (site approval) with the site map and lease. SEDD says it usually takes 3 to 5 working days
  5. Other approvals if your activity needs them, for example a health permit for a restaurant or Civil Defence for industry
  6. Submit and pay: SEDD issues a payment voucher, and the licence is sent after payment

SEDD says licences with no outside approvals can be issued within a day; licences that need approvals take about 5 to 7 working days.

Documents

  • Passport and Emirates ID copies of each owner or partner, and personal photos
  • Trade name certificate and technical evaluation approval
  • Attested lease contract (or title deed)
  • Memorandum of Association for companies
  • Approvals for your activity, if needed
  • If you work for a government entity in Sharjah: a no-objection letter

After the licence is issued, a non-resident owner needs an investor residence visa. Remember the licence must be renewed every year. See trade licence renewal.

Questions

New trade licence (Sharjah): common questions

What are the steps to get a trade licence in Sharjah?

SEDD's steps are: trade name, initial approval, an attested lease contract, technical evaluation of the site, other approvals if needed, then submit and pay.

Can a foreigner own 100% of a business in Sharjah?

SEDD says foreigners can own 100% of most mainland businesses since 2021, but some activities are reserved for UAE citizens.

How long does a new Sharjah trade licence take?

SEDD says licences with no outside approvals can be issued within a day. Licences that need approvals take about 5 to 7 working days.

What is the technical evaluation for a Sharjah licence?

It is SEDD's site approval. You submit the site map and lease contract, and it usually takes 3 to 5 working days.

What types of trade licence does Sharjah issue?

SEDD lists commercial, professional, industrial, e-commerce and Eitimad (home business for UAE citizens) licences.
