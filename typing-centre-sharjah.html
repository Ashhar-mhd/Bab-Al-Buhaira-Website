What is a typing centre, and what does it cost?

Most UAE government applications for visas, Emirates ID, labour and attestation can be prepared and submitted for you by a typing centre. Here is what a typing centre does, how the price is made up, and how we work.

Ask on WhatsApp View Government typing service

What does a typing centre do?

A typing centre prepares and submits government applications for you. Instead of learning each government website or app, you bring or send your documents, and the typing centre fills in the application, uploads the documents, pays the government fee and follows up until it is done.

Common services at a typing centre

  • Visas: family visa, visit visa, residence visa renewal and cancellation, Golden Visa.
  • Emirates ID: new card, renewal, replacement and medical fitness test booking.
  • Labour (MOHRE): labour contracts, work permits, labour cancellation and complaints.
  • Attestation and translation: MOFA attestation and legal translation.
  • Police services: police clearance certificate.
  • Company work: trade licence, establishment card and PRO services.

Why use a typing centre?

  • Fewer mistakes: a wrong entry or a missing document can mean rejection and paying again.
  • Saves time: you send clear photos on WhatsApp instead of visiting several offices.
  • One place: visa, Emirates ID, medical and insurance for the same person can be handled together.

What does it cost?

The total has two parts:

  • Government fee: set by the authority (ICP, MOHRE, MOFA and others) and the same wherever you apply.
  • Service charge: what the typing centre charges for its work.

The price depends on the service, so there is no single cost. Before you pay, ask for the total with the government fee and the service charge listed separately. We always give it to you like that.

What to bring or send

  • Passport copy
  • Emirates ID copy (if you have one)
  • For company services: trade licence and establishment card

We tell you the exact list for your service before you start.
