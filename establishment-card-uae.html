Establishment card for your UAE company

Opening a company in the UAE? One of the ICP steps is the establishment card. The card holds your company's details: trade name, licence number, partners and activity. Here is what ICP asks for.

Ask on WhatsApp View Government typing service

What the establishment card is

The establishment card is an electronic card issued by ICP (Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs and Port Security). It records your company's data, such as the trade name, licence number, partners and type of activity.

Conditions

  • A valid trade licence. See new trade licence in Sharjah
  • A valid Emirates ID for one of the authorised signatories. A visitor without an Emirates ID can use the Unified Number
  • Applications go only through authorised service providers
  • Free zone companies apply through their own free zone authority

Documents for a private company

  • Trade licence (for an LLC, a copy showing the partners)
  • Signature authorisation letter
  • Memorandum of Association (MOA), if there are partners
  • Any papers the emirate's licensing authority asks for

Steps

  1. Submit the application (ICP smart services, the ICP app, or a service centre)
  2. Check the company data pulled from the licence and correct it if needed
  3. Attach documents if the data cannot be pulled automatically
  4. Pay the fees
  5. ICP approves and issues the electronic card

ICP says the service takes about 2 days. The card is renewed through ICP's separate renewal service.

Fines ICP lists

  • AED 2,000 for breaking ICP's instructions or misusing smart services
  • AED 20,000 if entry permits are issued for a company that is not doing any business

Questions

Establishment card: common questions

What is an establishment card in the UAE?

It is an electronic card from ICP that records your company's data, such as trade name, licence number, partners and activity.

What do I need for an establishment card?

A valid trade licence, the signatory's Emirates ID, a signature authorisation letter and the MOA if there are partners.

How long does the establishment card take?

ICP says about 2 days after the application is complete.

How does a free zone company get an establishment card?

ICP says free zone companies apply through the free zone authority their company belongs to.
