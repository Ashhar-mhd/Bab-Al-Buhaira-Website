Residence visa renewal in the UAE

Your residence visa must be renewed before you run out of time after it expires. Here is what ICP asks for, how long the grace period is for your visa type, and how we handle it for you.

How residence renewal works

ICP's "Renewal of residency permits" service renews your residence together with your Emirates ID. All the conditions you met for the first visa must still be met when you renew. ICP lists the service as taking about 2 days once the application is complete.

What ICP usually asks for

  • Passport valid for at least 6 months
  • A recent photo with a white background, face clearly visible
  • Valid health insurance
  • Medical fitness report, for the visa types that need it
  • For family visas: attested marriage certificate for a spouse, birth certificates for children
  • For some visa types: tenancy contract and a water or electricity bill
  • For employees: the work permit from MOHRE, or the employment contract for public sector jobs

The exact list depends on your visa type, so we check it for you before we start.

Grace period after expiry

After your residence expires or is cancelled, ICP gives a grace period that depends on the visa type:

  • 180 days: Golden, Green and Blue residence holders and their families, widows or divorced women of residents, students after finishing their studies, and relatives of UAE citizens with a foreign passport
  • 90 days: skilled workers in levels 1 to 3, and property owners
  • 60 days: residence issued with a guarantor or host
  • 30 days: all other residence types

If you stay in the UAE after the grace period ends without renewing, ICP charges a fine of AED 50 per day. See our overstay fines guide to check and clear fines.

Don't forget the Emirates ID

The Emirates ID is renewed with the residence. For more on the card, see our Emirates ID renewal guide.

We tell you the exact total before you pay, with the government fee and our service charge listed separately.

Questions

Residence visa renewal: common questions

What do I need to renew my UAE residence visa?

Usually a passport valid for at least 6 months, a photo, valid health insurance and, for some visa types, a medical report, tenancy contract or family documents. All the original visa conditions must still be met.

How long is the grace period after my residence visa expires?

It depends on the visa type: 180 days for Golden, Green and Blue residence and some family cases, 90 days for skilled workers in levels 1 to 3 and property owners, 60 days for visas with a guarantor or host, and 30 days for all others.

What is the fine for not renewing my residence visa?

ICP charges AED 50 per day for staying in the UAE after the grace period ends without renewing.

Is the Emirates ID renewed with the residence visa?

Yes. ICP renews the residence together with the Emirates ID card.
