Absconding case in the UAE: how to check and remove it

An absconding case can stop you from getting a new job in the UAE. Here is how it works, how to check if you have one, and when it can be cancelled. Message us early and we check your case.

What is an absconding case?

"Absconding" is the common name for an absence from work complaint. A private-sector employer files it with MOHRE (Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation) when a worker stops coming to work without a valid reason.

The employer cannot file it straight away. Under MOHRE rules, the worker must have been absent without a lawful reason for more than 7 days in a row, and the employer must not know where the worker is or be able to contact them.

How to check if you have an absconding case

  • Call the MOHRE contact centre on 600 590000 (8 am to 8 pm, several languages).
  • Or send us your passport copy and Emirates ID (or labour card) on WhatsApp, and we check your status with MOHRE for you.

When can it be cancelled?

MOHRE can cancel the complaint, at the request of the worker or the employer, in cases like these:

  • The worker was absent for less than 7 days.
  • The worker was actually at work or on approved leave.
  • The employer agrees to take the worker back (the employer settles any fines).
  • The absence was agreed, for example to end the job or to look for new work.
  • There was a valid reason for the absence, or there is other proof the complaint is wrong.

Keep any proof you have: attendance records, leave approvals, messages with your employer and salary records. If you think the complaint is wrong, you can file a grievance with MOHRE with your documents. We can type and submit it for you.

What happens if the case is proven?

The main result is a one-year ban on a new work permit. The ban starts when you leave the UAE and ends on its own after one year. Some people are not affected, for example workers on a family member's residence visa, workers going back to the same company, Golden Visa holders and some skilled categories.

Questions

Absconding case: common questions

How do I know if I have an absconding case?

Call the MOHRE contact centre on 600 590000, or send us your passport copy and Emirates ID on WhatsApp and we check with MOHRE for you.

When can my employer file an absconding case?

Only after you have been absent without a valid reason for more than 7 days in a row, and the employer cannot reach you or does not know where you are.

Can an absconding case be removed?

Yes, in some cases. MOHRE can cancel it if you were absent less than 7 days, were really at work or on leave, the employer agrees to take you back, the absence was agreed, or there was a valid reason. We can help you file the request.

What happens if an absconding case is proven?

You get a one-year ban on a new work permit. It starts when you leave the UAE and ends on its own. Some people, like those on a family member's visa or Golden Visa holders, are not affected.
