Saudi visit visa from the UAE

Planning a trip to Saudi Arabia or an Umrah from the UAE? Here is how the Saudi tourist visa works, what we need from you, and what it costs. We apply for you.

Ask on WhatsApp View Visas for other countries service

Which Saudi visa do UAE residents use?

Most visitors from the UAE travel on the Saudi tourist e-visa. It is used for holidays, visiting family and friends, and Umrah. Whether you can apply depends on your nationality and your UAE residence, so send us your passport and we check this before anything is paid.

What the tourist e-visa gives you

  • Validity: one year, multiple entry, with up to 90 days in Saudi Arabia (as stated by Visit Saudi).
  • Umrah: allowed on the tourist visa, but not Hajj.
  • Usual time: about 1 working day after we submit.

Umrah on a tourist visa

You can perform Umrah on the tourist visa. You also need an Umrah permit, booked on the official Saudi app. We tell you the steps when your visa is ready.

GCC citizens

Citizens of GCC countries, including UAE nationals, do not need a visa for Saudi Arabia. A valid passport is enough.

What to send us

  • Passport copy, valid for more than 6 months
  • UAE residence visa and Emirates ID copy
  • Passport photo with a white background

Travelling as a family?

Send the passports of everyone travelling and we apply for all of them together. We can also book your flight and hotel at the same time.

Saudi visit visa from AED 495, including our service charge. We confirm the exact total for your passport before you pay.

Saudi visit visa: common questions

Can UAE residents get a Saudi visit visa?

Many UAE residents can apply for the Saudi tourist e-visa, depending on nationality and residence. Send us your passport and UAE visa and we check before you pay.

Can I do Umrah on a Saudi tourist visa?

Yes. Visit Saudi says Umrah is allowed on the tourist visa, but not Hajj.

How long is the Saudi tourist visa valid?

Visit Saudi says the e-visa is valid for one year with multiple entries, and you can stay up to 90 days in Saudi Arabia.

How much is a Saudi visit visa from the UAE?

From AED 495 with us, including our service charge. We confirm the exact total for your passport before you pay.

How long does the Saudi visa take?

Usually about 1 working day after we submit the application.
