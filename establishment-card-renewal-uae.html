Establishment card: renewal, changes and cancellation

Your company's establishment card must stay valid and up to date. ICP has separate services to renew it, change its details and cancel it. Here is what each one needs.

Ask on WhatsApp View Government typing service

Renewing the card

New company? First see how to get the establishment card. To renew, ICP (Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs and Port Security) asks for:

  • A valid trade licence. Renew it first if needed, see trade licence renewal
  • Payment of any fines on the company
  • Trade licence, signature authorisation letter and the MOA if there are partners

ICP says renewal takes about 2 days.

Late renewal fine

If the card is renewed more than 30 days after it expires, ICP charges AED 100 for each month of delay, up to AED 1,000.

Changing the card's details

ICP's amendment service covers changes such as a new trade name, transfer of ownership or adding or removing a partner. You need the updated licence, a letter from the authorised person to ICP, and any fines paid. ICP says it takes about 2 days.

Tell ICP about changes. ICP's fine for changing company information without informing it is AED 1,000. Changed your trade name? See changing your trade name in Sharjah.

Cancelling the card

  • Cancel the trade licence first
  • Pay all fines and clear all workers
  • Apply online with the cancelled licence copy. ICP says it takes about 2 days

Questions

Establishment card renewal: common questions

What is the fine for late establishment card renewal?

ICP charges AED 100 per month if renewed more than 30 days after expiry, up to AED 1,000.

What do I need to renew an establishment card?

A valid trade licence, the signature authorisation letter, the MOA if there are partners, and any fines paid.

Do I need to update the establishment card after changing partners or trade name?

Yes. ICP's amendment service covers these changes, and the fine for not informing ICP is AED 1,000.

How do I cancel an establishment card?

Cancel the trade licence first, pay all fines and clear all workers, then apply to ICP.
