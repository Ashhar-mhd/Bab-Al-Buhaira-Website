Golden Visa for entrepreneurs

ICP gives a 10-year Golden Visa to entrepreneurs with established businesses or successful business plans, inside or outside the UAE. Here are ICP's three routes, and we can check your case and apply for you.

Ask on WhatsApp View Golden Visa service

ICP's three routes for entrepreneurs

You need to meet one of these, according to ICP's Golden Residency guide:

  1. Existing UAE business: you own, or are a partner in, a business registered in the UAE as a small or medium enterprise (SME), in an approved field, with annual revenue of at least AED 1,000,000.
  2. Approved business idea: your business idea is approved by an accredited business incubator, the Ministry of Economy, ICP or a relevant local authority.
  3. Business already sold: you founded, or co-founded, one or more businesses that were sold for a total of at least AED 7,000,000.

Health insurance

ICP says you and your family members must have comprehensive, valid health insurance when you apply.

What you get

  • A 10-year residence that renews, without needing a sponsor
  • Residence for your spouse, children of any age, and parents
  • You can sponsor domestic workers
  • If the holder dies, family members can stay until their permits expire

Applying from outside the UAE

ICP says eligible applicants outside the UAE can get a six-month multiple-entry visa, renewable once for six more months, to finish the Golden Visa procedures.

What to send us

  • Passport copy and photo with a white background
  • Trade licence and proof of annual revenue, or the approval letter for your business idea, or proof of the business sale
  • Health insurance for you and your family

Questions

Golden Visa for entrepreneurs: common questions

Can a business owner get the UAE Golden Visa?

Yes, under ICP's entrepreneur category. One route is owning or being a partner in a UAE-registered SME in an approved field with annual revenue of at least AED 1,000,000.

Can I get a Golden Visa with only a business idea?

ICP has a route for entrepreneurs whose business idea is approved by an accredited business incubator, the Ministry of Economy, ICP or a relevant local authority.

How long is the Golden Visa for entrepreneurs?

ICP gives entrepreneurs a 10-year residence that renews, without needing a sponsor.

Can an entrepreneur Golden Visa holder sponsor family?

Yes. ICP says the holder can get residence for the spouse, children of any age, and parents.
