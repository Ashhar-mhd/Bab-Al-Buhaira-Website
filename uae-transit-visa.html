UAE transit visa: 48 and 96 hours

Stopping in the UAE between flights? ICP has short transit visas for passengers on the way to another country, and an emergency visa if your flight is cancelled. Here are the rules.

Transit visa types

  • 96-hour transit visa: through UAE airports and sea ports
  • 48-hour transit visa: through sea ports

ICP (Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs and Port Security) says the transit visa is issued through the airlines. So ask your airline about it when you book.

Conditions

  • A valid passport or travel document
  • A ticket to continue your journey to another country (except a seafarer joining a ship in a UAE port)
  • A personal photo

Emergency visa

If a transit passenger must stay more than 24 hours because of a flight cancellation due to weather, a sudden aircraft fault or a medical emergency, ICP grants an emergency visa. It is issued through the airline and ICP says it is free of charge.

  • Flight cancelled or aircraft fault: stay up to 96 hours
  • Medical emergency: stay up to 7 days. A longer stay must be changed to a medical treatment visa

Do not overstay

ICP's fine is AED 50 for each day after the visa expires or is cancelled. See overstay fines.

Want to stay longer than a layover? A normal visit visa is the better choice.

Questions

UAE transit visa: common questions

How long is a UAE transit visa?

ICP has a 96-hour transit visa through airports and sea ports, and a 48-hour transit visa through sea ports.

How do I get a UAE transit visa?

ICP says the transit visa is issued through the airlines. You need a valid passport and a ticket to continue your journey.

What if my flight is cancelled in the UAE?

If you must stay more than 24 hours because of weather or an aircraft fault, ICP grants a free emergency visa through the airline, for up to 96 hours.

Can I stay longer on a transit visa?

No. For a longer stay, apply for a visit visa instead.
