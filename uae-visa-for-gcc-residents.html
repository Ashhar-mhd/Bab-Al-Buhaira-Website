UAE visa for GCC residents

Family or friends living in Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Qatar, Oman or Bahrain? Foreign residents of GCC countries have their own UAE entry visa from ICP. Here is how long it lasts and what it needs.

Ask on WhatsApp View Visit visa service

Who this visa is for

ICP (Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs and Port Security) lists two GCC groups:

  • GCC residents: foreigners who hold a residence permit in a GCC country
  • Foreign companions of GCC citizens: such as the wife, children and domestic workers travelling with them

ICP's conditions also look at the residence permit's period and the person's profession or job level, so we check eligibility before applying.

How long it lasts

  • GCC residents: the visa is valid for 60 days from issue, so the person must enter within that time. The stay is 30 days, and it can be extended once for another 30 days
  • Companions of GCC citizens: the stay is 60 days, and it can be extended up to a total of 120 days

Documents

  • Passport
  • A recent photo with a white background, face clearly visible
  • GCC residence permit

Steps

  1. Apply through ICP smart services or the ICP app
  2. Check the details and attach documents if needed
  3. Pay the fees and receive the electronic visa

ICP says the service takes about 2 days.

Do not overstay

ICP's fine is AED 50 for each day of stay after the visa expires or is cancelled. See overstay fines.

Questions

UAE visa for GCC residents: common questions

Can a GCC resident get a UAE visa?

Yes. ICP has an entry visa for foreigners who hold a residence permit in a GCC country. Eligibility also depends on the residence and the person's job.

How long can a GCC resident stay in the UAE?

30 days, and the stay can be extended once for another 30 days. The visa must be used within 60 days of issue.

How long can a foreign companion of a GCC citizen stay?

ICP allows 60 days, extendable up to 120 days in total.

What documents does a GCC resident need for a UAE visa?

Passport, photo and the GCC residence permit.
