Exit permit cancellation in the UAE

An exit permit lets a person leave the UAE. If your plans change and you want to stay legally, the exit permit has to be cancelled with ICP. Here is how it works.

Ask on WhatsApp View UAE visas service

What is exit permit cancellation?

ICP describes it as a service to cancel an exit permit that was issued before. It is linked to ICP's departure permit service, and it is open to both residents and visitors.

The main condition

ICP's terms say the person's status in the UAE must be amended. In simple words, you cancel the exit permit when you are staying on, for example on a new visit visa, employment visa or family visa, instead of leaving.

Don't wait too long

ICP lists a daily fine starting from the 8th day after the exit permit expires, or after it is cancelled. Fines for visa and residence violations have to be paid before the status is fixed or before you leave. See overstay fines for how these fines work.

Documents

  • Passport copy
  • A recent photo with a white background, face clearly visible

Steps

  1. Submit the application on ICP Smart Services (website or app, with UAE Pass)
  2. Review the details and update them if needed
  3. Attach the documents if ICP does not already have them
  4. Pay the fees
  5. ICP approves the application and cancels the exit permit

ICP lists the completion time as 2 days.

Questions

Exit permit cancellation: common questions

Can I cancel an exit permit in the UAE?

Yes. ICP has an Exit Permit Cancellation service for residents and visitors. The condition is that your status in the UAE is amended, for example to a new visa.

How long does exit permit cancellation take?

ICP lists a completion time of 2 days.

What documents are needed to cancel an exit permit?

A passport copy and a personal photo. We use a recent photo with a white background, face clearly visible.

Is there a fine after an exit permit expires?

Yes. ICP lists a daily fine starting from the 8th day after the exit permit expires or is cancelled. Fines must be paid before the status is fixed or before leaving.
