UAE Golden Visa eligibility: who qualifies and what to prepare

The UAE Golden Visa is a long-term, renewable residence visa valid for 5 or 10 years, depending on your category. You sponsor yourself, so you do not need an employer. This guide explains who can apply and what to prepare.

Ask on WhatsApp All guides

Who can apply?

The government groups applicants into categories. The main ones are:

  • Investors in public investments or businesses
  • Real estate investors who own one or more properties worth at least AED 2 million, where the property is not under a loan
  • Entrepreneurs with an approved business or project
  • Exceptional talents and specialists, such as doctors, scientists, engineers and executives who meet the conditions
  • Outstanding students and graduates from UAE schools and universities
  • Humanitarian pioneers and frontline heroes

Each category has its own conditions, such as salary, experience or grades, and they are updated from time to time. That is why we check your details against the current rules before you pay anything.

What are the benefits?

  • Residence for 5 or 10 years, and it can be renewed
  • No employer sponsor needed
  • You can sponsor family members, including your spouse and children
  • You can stay outside the UAE for longer than the usual six months without losing your residence

Documents to prepare

  • Passport and current visa (if you have one)
  • Emirates ID (if you live in the UAE)
  • Passport photo with a white background
  • Proof for your category, for example a title deed, salary certificate, work contract, degree or grade certificate
  • Attested degree certificate where your category needs one

How to check if you qualify

Send us your category details on WhatsApp: for example your property value, salary and job title, or your qualifications. We tell you which category fits, which papers are missing, and the full price with government fees and our service charge listed separately.
