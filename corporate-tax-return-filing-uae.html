Filing your UAE Corporate Tax return

Every business registered for UAE Corporate Tax must file a return, even with low or no income. Here is the FTA's deadline rule, what goes in the return and what to prepare.

Who must file

  • All taxable persons registered for Corporate Tax, whatever their income
  • Businesses using Small Business Relief (they file a simplified return)
  • Exempt persons who are required to register file an annual declaration instead

The deadline: 9 months

The FTA says the return must be filed, and the tax paid, within 9 months from the end of your tax period (your financial year).

  • Financial year ended 31 December 2025: deadline was 30 September 2026 (FTA)
  • Financial year ending 31 December 2026: the same rule gives 30 September 2027

Not sure of your tax period? Log in to EmaraTax and check it against your financial year.

Pay early

The FTA warns that bank transfers can take time, and a payment that arrives after the deadline counts as late. Filing and paying do not have to happen on the same day, so pay well before the deadline.

What the return contains

  • Tax period, name, address and Tax Registration Number (TRN)
  • The accounting standards used in your financial statements
  • Taxable income, tax losses used and carried forward, and tax credits
  • The Corporate Tax payable

What to prepare

  • Trade licence and EmaraTax login
  • Financial statements and accounting records for the year
  • Details of your business activities

The FTA says you must keep records that support your return, including your revenue and taxable income. Missing records bring administrative penalties, and late filing or late payment brings penalties too.

Returns are filed on EmaraTax, by you or through an FTA-registered tax agent. We help you prepare the papers and file on EmaraTax.

Questions

Corporate Tax return filing: common questions

What is the deadline to file a UAE Corporate Tax return?

Within 9 months from the end of your tax period. For a financial year ending 31 December, that is 30 September of the next year.

Do I need to file a Corporate Tax return if I have no profit?

Yes. The FTA says all taxable persons must file, whatever their level of income.

Do I need to file if I use Small Business Relief?

Yes. Businesses using Small Business Relief still file, using a simplified return.

Where do I file the Corporate Tax return?

On the FTA's EmaraTax platform, yourself or through an FTA-registered tax agent.

What happens if I pay Corporate Tax late?

The FTA says late filing and late payment bring penalties. A bank transfer that arrives after the deadline counts as late, so pay early.
