SEWA Move In: getting electricity and water in Sharjah

Moving into a flat or shop in Sharjah? You open your electricity and water account with SEWA (Sharjah Electricity, Water and Gas Authority) through its online Move In service. Here is what SEWA asks for.

Ask on WhatsApp View Tenancy & utilities service

Step 1: attest your tenancy contract

SEWA's Move In form says to start with tenancy contract attestation before applying. See tenancy contract attestation in Sharjah.

Step 2: apply online

  1. Choose the type of place: residential or commercial / industrial
  2. Verify yourself with your Emirates ID or UAE Pass, and say if you are the owner or a tenant
  3. Enter the electricity meter number (or consumer number) and the tenancy contract number
  4. Upload the documents and confirm

SEWA says the new connection process is done on its online portal, without visiting a SEWA office. You get a service request number after you submit.

Documents SEWA's form asks for

  • Emirates ID (front and back)
  • Tenancy contract, both sides in one file
  • Photos of the electricity and water meters (and the gas meter, if there is one)
  • For shops and companies: trade licence and the economic department's inspection report
  • Other papers in some cases, such as a no-objection certificate or the ownership deed

Files must be clear PDF or JPG, one file per document, each up to 5 MB.

Before you start

  • Your mobile number must be linked to your Emirates ID. If it is not, see how to update it
  • SEWA's form includes a declaration that you will not rent out the premises

SEWA's online pages do not list the deposit amount. We confirm the deposit and total for you before applying.

Questions

SEWA Move In: common questions

How do I get electricity and water for a new flat in Sharjah?

Apply for SEWA's Move In service online, after attesting your tenancy contract. You verify with Emirates ID or UAE Pass and upload your documents.

What documents does SEWA need for Move In?

SEWA's form asks for your Emirates ID, the tenancy contract (both sides in one file) and photos of the meters. Shops also need the trade licence and inspection report.

Do I need to attest my tenancy contract before SEWA Move In?

Yes. SEWA's Move In form says to start with tenancy contract attestation before applying.

Why does SEWA Move In fail with my mobile number?

SEWA needs a mobile number linked to your Emirates ID. If yours is not linked, update it with ICP first.
