Visit visas we handle

We apply for UAE visit and tourist visas for relatives and friends of UAE residents, and for visitors coming on their own. Short and longer stay options are available, as single or multiple entry, depending on the current rules and the visitor's nationality.

We also handle visa extensions and change of status for visitors already in the UAE when it is allowed.

Documents to prepare

  • Visitor's passport copy, valid 6+ months
  • Passport photo, white background
  • Sponsor's passport, visa and Emirates ID (for relative visas)
  • Relationship proof for some relative visas
  • Return ticket or hotel booking if required

What we do for you

  • Advise the right visa type and duration
  • Apply and follow up the application
  • Send the e-visa to you on WhatsApp
  • Extension or change of status when allowed
  • Air tickets on request

Visit visa prices depend on the duration and nationality. Ask on WhatsApp and we send you the current price before applying.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

How long does a UAE visit visa take?

Many visit visas are issued within a few working days, and some sooner. Timing depends on the visitor's nationality and any extra checks. We tell you the expected time before applying.

Can I extend a visit visa inside the UAE?

Some visit visas can be extended without leaving the country. Message us with the visa copy and we check the options.

Do I need to come to the office?

No. Visit visas can be done fully on WhatsApp. Send clear photos of the documents and we handle the rest.

Can a visit visa be changed to a residence visa?

In many cases a visitor can change status to a family or employment visa without leaving the UAE. We can arrange this.
