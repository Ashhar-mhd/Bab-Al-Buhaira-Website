Emirates ID · Residence · ICP

No visa sticker in your passport? Your Emirates ID is your residence proof

Got a new or renewed UAE residence visa and no sticker in your passport? That is normal. ICP stopped issuing the residence sticker, and your Emirates ID is now the proof that you live in the UAE.

Ask on WhatsApp View Emirates ID service

What changed

In April 2022, ICP (Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs and Port Security) decided to stop issuing the residence sticker in the passports of foreign residents. The Emirates ID card replaced it as proof of residence.

ICP also merged the two applications. The residence visa and the Emirates ID are now applied for in one application, which ICP says saves time, effort and cost.

Why the Emirates ID is enough

ICP says the new generation of Emirates ID cards for residents holds all the details that were on the residence sticker: your personal and work details and the issuing details, stored in the card's chip.

What this means for you

  • Keep your Emirates ID valid. It is your residence proof, so renew it on time. See Emirates ID renewal
  • Lost it? Apply for a replacement quickly. See lost Emirates ID
  • Need a digital copy? ICP's smart app lets you get an electronic copy of your Emirates ID
  • Need your visa details on paper for an embassy, bank or employer? Ask for a residence details report

Visa sticker and Emirates ID: common questions

Why is there no visa sticker in my passport?

ICP stopped issuing the residence sticker in April 2022. Your Emirates ID is now your proof of UAE residence.

Is the Emirates ID proof of UAE residence?

Yes. ICP says the new Emirates ID cards hold all the details that were on the residence sticker.

Do I apply for the residence visa and Emirates ID separately?

No. ICP merged them into one application.

How do I show my residence visa details on paper?

You can get a residence details report from ICP, or an electronic copy of your Emirates ID from the ICP app. We can do this for you.
