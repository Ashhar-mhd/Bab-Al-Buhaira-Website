Who can you sponsor on a UAE family visa?

Before you start a family visa, check who you can sponsor and whether your salary is enough. Here are the official rules, and we can handle the whole file for you.

Ask on WhatsApp View Family visa service

Family members you can sponsor

According to the UAE Government portal, an expatriate resident can sponsor:

  • Your wife or husband
  • Your unmarried daughters
  • Your sons under 25 years old
  • Your children with special needs

Parents follow different rules. For a short stay, see our visit visa for relatives guide, or ask us about your case.

Salary you need

The sponsor needs a minimum salary of AED 4,000 a month, or AED 3,000 plus accommodation.

Medical test

Family members aged 18 or over must pass a medical fitness test at an approved health centre before the residence is issued.

If your family is outside the UAE

We first apply for an entry permit. ICP says the family member must enter the UAE within the 60-day validity of the permit, and can then stay up to 60 days from entry to complete the residence steps. If your family is already in the UAE, we can usually change their status inside the country.

What you, the sponsor, send us

  • Your passport copy with UAE visa, and your Emirates ID
  • Salary certificate or labour contract
  • Marriage certificate (for your spouse) or birth certificates (for children), attested where needed
  • Passport copies of your family members and a recent photo of each with a white background, face clearly visible

Questions

Who can I sponsor: common questions

Who can I sponsor on a UAE family visa?

An expatriate resident can sponsor a spouse, unmarried daughters, sons under 25 and children with special needs, according to the UAE Government portal.

What salary do I need to sponsor my family in the UAE?

A minimum monthly salary of AED 4,000, or AED 3,000 plus accommodation.

Can I sponsor my son if he is over 25?

The standard rule covers sons under 25 years old. Children with special needs can be sponsored at any age. Ask us and we check your case.

Does my family need a medical test for the visa?

Yes, family members aged 18 or over must pass a medical fitness test at an approved health centre.
