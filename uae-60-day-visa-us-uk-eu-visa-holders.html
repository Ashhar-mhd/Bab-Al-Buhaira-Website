UAE 60-day visa for holders of a US, UK or EU visa

ICP issues a 60-day UAE visa to nationals of certain countries who already hold a valid visa or residence from the US, UK, EU and some other countries. Here is who qualifies and what to send us.

Ask on WhatsApp View Visit visa service

Who can get this 60-day visa

ICP lists these nationals (ordinary passport holders) and their family members:

Indian nationals

  • Holding a valid US, EU or UK visa or residence permit, or a US Green Card, or
  • Holding a valid residence permit from Japan, South Korea, Australia, Canada, Singapore or New Zealand

Kenya, Indonesia, Vietnam, Thailand, the Philippines and South Africa

  • Holding a valid US, EU or UK visa or residence permit, or
  • Holding a valid residence permit from Japan, South Korea, Australia, Canada, Singapore or New Zealand

The passport must be valid for at least 6 months.

Stay rules

  • After the visa is issued, you must enter the UAE within 30 days
  • You can stay 60 days from the date of entry
  • ICP says this visa cannot be extended, so plan your exit date
  • Overstaying, or an expired entry visa, brings a fine of AED 50 per day

Need a longer stay? See the 5-year multiple-entry tourist visa or our visit visa options.

What to send us

  • Passport copy (valid 6 months or more)
  • A recent photo with a white background, face clearly visible
  • A copy of the valid US, UK or EU visa, residence permit or Green Card (or residence from Japan, South Korea, Australia, Canada, Singapore or New Zealand)

We apply through ICP for you. ICP lists the processing time as about 2 days.

We check your papers first and tell you the total cost before we apply.

Questions

60-day visa (US/UK/EU visa holders): common questions

Can Indians with a US visa get a UAE visa?

Yes. ICP issues a 60-day UAE visa to Indian ordinary passport holders and their family members who hold a valid US, EU or UK visa or residence permit, or a US Green Card.

Which countries' visas qualify for the UAE 60-day visa?

A valid visa or residence permit from the US, EU or UK, or a valid residence permit from Japan, South Korea, Australia, Canada, Singapore or New Zealand.

Which nationalities can get the UAE 60-day visa with a US, UK or EU visa?

ICP lists India, Kenya, Indonesia, Vietnam, Thailand, the Philippines and South Africa (ordinary passport holders and their families).

Can the UAE 60-day visa be extended?

No. ICP says this visa cannot be extended. The stay is 60 days from the date of entry.

How long do I have to enter the UAE on the 60-day visa?

ICP gives 30 days from issue to enter the UAE.
