Golden Visa for skilled professionals

Working in the UAE with a high salary or a senior role? You may qualify for the 10-year Golden Visa. Here are ICP's current rules for professionals, and we can check your case.

Ask on WhatsApp View Golden Visa service

Specialised professionals: ICP's conditions

ICP's Golden Residency Guide lists these conditions for the specialised professionals category:

  • A valid UAE work permit under a valid, approved employment contract
  • At least a bachelor's degree or equivalent
  • A monthly salary of at least AED 30,000, or the same in foreign currency

Senior roles

ICP also lists skilled professionals in senior occupational levels under the MOHRE classification: chairpersons and chief executives at occupational level one, and specialists at occupational level two. Your level is shown in your MOHRE work permit, so we check it for you.

How long is it valid?

ICP lists 10 years, with automatic renewal and no sponsor needed.

Your family

  • Residence for your spouse and children, whatever their age, and for your parents
  • You can sponsor domestic workers under the normal rules
  • You and your family need valid health insurance for the whole residence period

Check if you qualify

ICP has an online eligibility checker on its smart services platform. Or send us your details and we check it for you.

What to send us

  • Passport copy, UAE visa and Emirates ID
  • Labour contract and salary certificate
  • Degree certificate (attested, if needed)
  • A recent photo with a white background, face clearly visible

Questions

Golden Visa for professionals: common questions

What salary do I need for the Golden Visa as a professional?

ICP's guide lists a monthly salary of at least AED 30,000 for the specialised professionals category, plus a bachelor's degree and a valid UAE work permit.

Do I need a degree for the Golden Visa as a professional?

Yes. For the specialised professionals category, ICP's guide asks for at least a bachelor's degree or equivalent.

How long is the Golden Visa for professionals?

ICP lists 10 years, with automatic renewal and no sponsor needed.

Can I sponsor my family on a Golden Visa?

Yes. ICP's guide says Golden Residency holders can get residence for their spouse, children of any age and parents. Everyone needs valid health insurance.
