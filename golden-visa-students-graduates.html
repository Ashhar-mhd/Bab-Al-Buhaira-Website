Golden Visa for outstanding students and graduates

Outstanding school students and university graduates can qualify for the 10-year UAE Golden Visa. Here are ICP's grade and GPA rules, and we can check your papers and apply for you.

Ask on WhatsApp View Golden Visa service

High school students

  • Grade 12 certificate from a government school, or a private school licensed by the Ministry of Education or the local education authority
  • A minimum grade of 95%, or its equivalent
  • Certificate attested by the Ministry of Education

Graduates of UAE universities

  • Bachelor's, master's or doctoral degree from a university licensed by the Ministry of Education
  • University in Category A or Category B of the Ministry of Education classification
  • GPA of at least 3.5 out of 4.0 for Category A, or 3.8 out of 4.0 for Category B, or the equivalent
  • No more than 2 years since graduation
  • Degree attested by the Ministry of Education

Graduates of international universities

  • University ranked among the top 100 in the world, by the ranking the Ministry of Education recognises
  • Bachelor's degree with a cumulative GPA of at least 3.5, or the equivalent
  • No more than 2 years since graduation
  • Degree attested by the Ministry of Education

How long is it valid?

ICP lists 10 years for outstanding students and graduates. Golden Residency holders can also get residence for their family, and everyone needs valid health insurance.

What to send us

  • Passport copy, and UAE visa and Emirates ID if you have them
  • Grade 12 certificate or degree certificate, with transcript showing the grade or GPA
  • A recent photo with a white background, face clearly visible

We check your certificates against these rules first, and arrange the Ministry of Education attestation if it is still needed.

Questions

Golden Visa for students: common questions

What grade does a school student need for the Golden Visa?

ICP's guide asks for a minimum of 95% (or equivalent) in Grade 12, from a government school or a licensed private school, with the certificate attested by the Ministry of Education.

What GPA do UAE university graduates need for the Golden Visa?

At least 3.5 out of 4.0 for a Category A university, or 3.8 out of 4.0 for Category B, with no more than 2 years since graduation.

Can graduates of foreign universities get the Golden Visa?

Yes, if the university is in the world top 100 by the ranking the Ministry of Education recognises, the GPA is at least 3.5, and it is no more than 2 years since graduation.

How long is the Golden Visa for students and graduates?

ICP lists 10 years for outstanding students and graduates.
