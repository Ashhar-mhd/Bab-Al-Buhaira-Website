Golden Visa for specialized talents

ICP issues a visa to people with exceptional talents so they can complete the 10-year Golden Residence. Each category needs a recommendation or approval from a specific UAE authority. Here is which one you need.

Ask on WhatsApp View Golden Visa service

ICP's 5 talent categories and the approval each needs

  • Arts and culture: approval from the competent government cultural authority in the UAE
  • Innovators and inventors: approval from the Ministry of Economy or the competent local authority
  • Athletes: approval from the General Sports Authority or a local sports council
  • Digital technology talents: approval from the UAE Council for Artificial Intelligence and Digital Transactions
  • Exceptional talents: approval from a competent federal or local government authority

Conditions

  • Valid health insurance in the UAE
  • Passport valid for at least 6 months

Documents ICP asks for

  • Passport
  • A recent photo with a white background, face clearly visible
  • The recommendation or approval letter for your category

How it works

  1. Get the recommendation or approval from the authority for your category.
  2. The application is submitted on the ICP smart services website or app, with UAE Pass.
  3. The data is reviewed, documents attached and the fees paid.
  4. The visa is issued so you can complete the 10-year Golden Residence.

ICP lists the service as taking about 2 days once the application is complete. The approval from your category authority usually takes the longest, so start with that.

Questions

Golden Visa for talents: common questions

Can artists and athletes get the UAE Golden Visa?

Yes. ICP has a route for specialized talents, including arts and culture and athletes. Artists need approval from a UAE cultural authority, and athletes from the General Sports Authority or a local sports council.

Which approval do digital technology talents need for the Golden Visa?

ICP says digital technology talents need approval from the UAE Council for Artificial Intelligence and Digital Transactions.

What documents are needed for the talent Golden Visa?

ICP asks for your passport, a personal photo and the recommendation or approval for your category, plus valid UAE health insurance.

How long does the talent Golden Visa take?

ICP lists the visa service as taking about 2 days after a complete application. Getting the category approval first usually takes longer.
