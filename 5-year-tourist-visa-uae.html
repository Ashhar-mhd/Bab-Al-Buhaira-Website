UAE 5-year multiple-entry tourist visa

ICP's long-term tourist visa lets a visitor enter and leave the UAE many times over 5 years, without a new visa each trip. Here is what ICP lists and what to send us.

Ask on WhatsApp View Visit visa service

What the 5-year tourist visa is

ICP issues a long-term multiple-entry tourist visa valid for 5 years. The holder can enter and exit the UAE many times during the validity, within ICP's limits on how long each stay can be.

Main conditions ICP lists

  • Passport valid for at least 6 months
  • A bank statement showing a balance of USD 4,000 (or the same in another currency) during the 6 months before applying
  • Health insurance issued in the UAE, valid for 180 days
  • A return ticket and proof of where you will stay in the UAE

How long you can stay

  • Each visit can be up to 90 days in a row; ICP can extend the stay
  • The yearly stay limit counts from the date of your first entry, not the calendar year
  • Unused days in one year do not carry over to the next year

ICP's page words the yearly limit in two ways, so we confirm the current limit for you before you travel.

Enter within 60 days

After the visa is issued, the first entry must be within 60 days, or the visa is cancelled or must be extended. This applies only to the first entry; after that the visa is multiple-entry.

Financial guarantee

ICP takes a refundable financial guarantee with this visa. You can ask for the refund after the visa is cancelled or the visitor has left the UAE, as long as there are no residency violations. See our visa deposit refund guide.

What to send us

  • Passport copy (valid 6 months or more)
  • A recent photo with a white background, face clearly visible
  • Bank statement for the last 6 months
  • Return ticket and proof of stay (hotel booking or host's address)
  • For nationals of Afghanistan, Iran or Iraq: a copy of the national ID card

We arrange the UAE health insurance and apply through ICP for you. ICP lists the processing time as about 2 days.

We check your papers first and tell you the total cost before we apply.

Questions

5-year tourist visa: common questions

How long is the UAE multiple-entry tourist visa valid?

ICP issues the long-term multiple-entry tourist visa for 5 years. The holder can enter and leave the UAE many times during that period.

What bank balance is needed for the UAE 5-year tourist visa?

ICP asks for a bank statement showing a balance of USD 4,000 or the same in another currency during the 6 months before applying.

How many days can I stay on each visit with the 5-year tourist visa?

ICP allows up to 90 days in a row per visit, and the stay can be extended by ICP. The yearly limit counts from your first entry date.

Do I need health insurance for the 5-year tourist visa?

Yes. ICP requires health insurance issued in the UAE, valid for 180 days.

When must I enter the UAE after the 5-year tourist visa is issued?

The first entry must be within 60 days of issue, or the visa is cancelled or must be extended. After the first entry this rule no longer applies.
