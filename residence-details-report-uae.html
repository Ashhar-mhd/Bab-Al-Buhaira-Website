Residence details report from ICP

ICP has a service that prints a report with your residence details and file information. It is for residents with an active residence file. Here is what ICP lists and how we can get it for you.

Ask on WhatsApp View Government typing service

What the report is

ICP describes it as a service that prints a report with your residence details and file information. People often ask for a printed record of their residence, for example for an office, a school or an embassy. Check with them that this ICP report is the paper they want.

Who can get it

  • ICP lists the service for residents
  • You must have an active residence file (a valid UAE residence)
  • The online service needs a UAE Pass login, or it can be done at an ICP customer happiness centre

What to send us

  • Passport copy
  • A recent photo with a white background, face clearly visible
  • Your Emirates ID copy
  • If your job has changed: your work card
  • If you work in the government sector, or are a student holding a MOHRE work card: a letter from the relevant government authority

How it works

  1. We submit the request on ICP Smart Services
  2. We check the details ICP pulls from your file and correct them if needed
  3. The fees are paid and the report is issued

If the details in your file are wrong, the report will be wrong too. See amending residence visa details first.

We check your papers first and tell you the total cost before we apply.

Last checked 21 November 2026 against ICP (icp.gov.ae, Residence details report, service code 377-006-003-008). Government rules can change, so we confirm everything before you pay.

Questions

Residence details report: common questions

What is the ICP residence details report?

It is an ICP service that prints a report containing your residence details and file information.

Who can get a residence details report in the UAE?

ICP lists the service for residents. You must have an active residence file.

Can I get the residence details report online?

Yes. It is on ICP Smart Services with a UAE Pass login, or at an ICP customer happiness centre. We can apply for you.

What documents are needed for the residence details report?

A passport and a photo. ICP also lists a work card if your job changed, and a government letter for government-sector workers or students holding a MOHRE work card.
