Residence visa cancellation in the UAE

Leaving the UAE, changing jobs or moving your family to a new sponsor? Your residence visa needs to be cancelled correctly to avoid fines. Here is how it works, and we can do it for you.

Ask on WhatsApp View UAE visas service

When do you need visa cancellation?

  • Leaving the UAE permanently: you are going back home or moving to another country and will not live in the UAE on this visa again.
  • Changing your sponsor: moving from one company to another company, or from your company visa to a family visa (sponsored by your husband, wife or parent).
  • Your family member is leaving or changing sponsor: you sponsor your wife, husband, children or parents, and they are leaving the UAE permanently or moving to another sponsor, for example to their own company visa.

In Sharjah and the other emirates outside Dubai, residence visas are handled by ICP.

Who applies

  • Employees: the cancellation is done by your employer.
  • Family members: the cancellation is done by the sponsor (husband, wife or parent).

A family member's residence is linked to the sponsor's and cannot be longer than it, so if the sponsor's visa is being cancelled, the family's visas need to be sorted out too. We check this for you before anything is submitted.

Grace period and fines

After cancellation you get a grace period to leave the UAE or move to a new visa. The length depends on your visa type, so we confirm it for your case. If you stay after the grace period ends, ICP charges an overstay fine of AED 50 per day.

Changing to a new visa without leaving

If you have a new sponsor or job, you can usually change your status inside the UAE instead of exiting and coming back. We tell you if this works for your case and apply for it.

What to send us

  • Passport copy with the current visa
  • Emirates ID copy
  • For family members: the sponsor's passport and Emirates ID copy

Government fees depend on the visa type. We tell you the exact total before you pay, with the government fee and our service charge listed separately.

Questions

Visa cancellation: common questions

Who cancels my residence visa?

For employees, the employer cancels it. For family members, the sponsor (husband, wife or parent) applies. We can do the typing and submission for you.

How long can I stay after my visa is cancelled?

There is a grace period to leave the UAE or change to a new visa. Its length depends on your visa type, so send us your details and we confirm it for you.

What is the fine if I stay after the grace period?

ICP charges an overstay fine of AED 50 per day after the grace period ends.

Can I change to a new visa without leaving the UAE?

Usually yes, with a status change inside the UAE. We check your case and apply for it.
