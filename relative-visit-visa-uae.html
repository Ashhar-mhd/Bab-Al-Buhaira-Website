Visit visa for your parents, relatives or friends

As a UAE resident you can sponsor a visit visa for your parents, other relatives or friends. The minimum salary you need depends on the relation. Here are ICP's rules, and we can apply for you.

Ask on WhatsApp View Visit visa service

Who you can sponsor, and the salary you need

ICP groups visitors by how closely they are related to you, the sponsor. Each group has its own minimum monthly salary:

  • First degree (father, mother, spouse, son, daughter): AED 4,000 a month
  • Second degree (brothers, sisters, grandparents, grandchildren): AED 8,000 a month
  • Third degree (uncle, aunt, cousins): AED 8,000 a month
  • Friend (not a relative): AED 15,000 a month

For relatives, you also need proof of the relationship, for example a birth or marriage certificate. You must have an active sponsor file with ICP.

How long they can stay

The visa is for 30, 60 or 90 days. ICP can extend it once or more, but the total stay in the UAE cannot be more than 120 days. For extensions, see our visit visa extension guide.

What the visitor needs

  • Passport valid for at least 6 months
  • A recent photo with a white background, face clearly visible
  • Health insurance valid in the UAE
  • Return ticket

What you, the sponsor, send us

  • Your passport copy with UAE visa, and your Emirates ID
  • Proof of salary, such as a salary certificate or labour contract
  • Proof of relationship for relatives
  • The visitor's passport copy and photo

ICP charges government fees and may ask for a refundable guarantee. We tell you the exact total before you pay, with the government fee and our service charge listed separately.

Last checked 11 November 2026 against ICP (icp.gov.ae, Issuance of a Visa service). Government rules can change, so we confirm everything before you pay.

Questions

Visa for relatives: common questions

What salary do I need to bring my parents on a visit visa?

ICP asks for a minimum monthly salary of AED 4,000 to sponsor first-degree relatives: father, mother, spouse, son or daughter.

Can I sponsor a visit visa for my brother or sister?

Yes. Brothers and sisters are second-degree relatives, and ICP asks for a minimum monthly salary of AED 8,000, plus proof of the relationship.

Can I sponsor a visit visa for a friend?

Yes. For a friend who is not a relative, ICP asks for a minimum monthly salary of AED 15,000.

How long can my relative stay on a visit visa?

The visa is for 30, 60 or 90 days. ICP can extend it, but the total stay cannot be more than 120 days.
