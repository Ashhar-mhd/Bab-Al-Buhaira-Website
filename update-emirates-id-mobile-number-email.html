Emirates ID · Data update · ICP

Change the mobile number, email or address on your Emirates ID

Changed your phone number? ICP's Data Update service lets you update the mobile number, email and address linked to your Emirates ID. Here is how it works, and how we can do it for you.

Ask on WhatsApp View Emirates ID service

What you can update

ICP's Data Update service changes the contact details linked to your Emirates ID:

  • Mobile number
  • Email address
  • Address

At least one of these must change to submit the request. ICP lists the service for UAE citizens, residents and GCC citizens. Other details, like your name or job, are not changed with this service. For residence details, see amending residence visa details.

How it works

  1. The request is made from your personal account on ICP Smart Services or the ICP app, with a UAE Pass login
  2. The new details are entered and the fees are paid
  3. ICP updates the details in its systems automatically

You confirm the request with your UAE Pass, and a one-time code (OTP) goes to the new number. So a lost or old SIM is not a problem.

Family members

ICP says family members' details are updated from the sponsor's or head of family's personal account. Send us the details for each family member and we do them together.

No UAE Pass?

Send us a copy of your Emirates ID on WhatsApp. We check your case and tell you how we can update your mobile number or email.

What to send us

  • Emirates ID copy
  • The new mobile number, email or address
  • Keep your phone with UAE Pass close, to confirm the request

We tell you the total cost before we start.

Questions

Update mobile, email or address: common questions

How do I change the mobile number on my Emirates ID?

Use ICP's Data Update service on ICP Smart Services or the ICP app with UAE Pass. You confirm with UAE Pass and an OTP goes to the new number. We can do it for you.

What details can I change with ICP's Data Update service?

ICP lists the mobile number, email address and address. At least one of them must change.

How do I update my family's Emirates ID mobile number?

ICP says family members' details are updated from the sponsor's or head of family's personal account.

Can I update my Emirates ID address online?

Yes. The address can be updated with ICP's Data Update service on ICP Smart Services or the ICP app, with a UAE Pass login.
