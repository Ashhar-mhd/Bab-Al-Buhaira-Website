      Questions

      Before you start

      Do I need to visit the office?

      Not always. Many services start on WhatsApp with photos of your documents. Medical tests and Emirates ID biometrics need you to attend in person at the government centre, and we tell you where and when.

      Are the calculator prices final?

      They are our current prices, including government fees and our service charge. Government fees can change without notice, so we confirm your final price before you pay anything.

      Do you handle visas for other emirates?

      Yes. We process applications for Sharjah, Dubai and the other emirates through the correct authority for each, such as ICP or GDRFA.

      Which languages can you translate?

      We arrange legal translation in more than 100 languages, including Arabic, English, Malayalam, Hindi, Urdu and Tamil. We also attest documents issued in over 100 countries.

      Are you a government office?

      No. Bab Al Buhaira is a private typing and services centre. We prepare and submit applications to government offices on your behalf.
