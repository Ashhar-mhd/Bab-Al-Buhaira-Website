Employment visa: finishing the residence after entry

When a new employee or domestic worker enters the UAE on an entry permit, the residence still has to be completed. ICP sets a deadline and a document list. Here is what employers and sponsors need to know.

Ask on WhatsApp View Government typing service

The 60-day deadline

ICP says the residence must be completed within 60 days of entry, or delay fines apply. ICP lists an overstay fine of AED 50 per day after the entry permit expires.

Which route applies

  • Companies under MOHRE: the residence is done through the Work Bundle.
  • Domestic workers sponsored by a UAE national or a resident: through the Domestic Workers Package.
  • Government, semi-government and free zone entities apply for their own employees.

Documents ICP asks for

  • Passport valid for at least 6 months
  • A recent photo with a white background, face clearly visible
  • Medical report (medical fitness)
  • One of these, depending on the employer: the MOHRE work permit, the employment contract or appointment decision, or the contract plus Wage Protection System proof

Already in the UAE? Change of status

ICP says that if the person is already in the UAE, the previous residence is cancelled and a change of status request is made inside the country, so the new residence can be completed without leaving the UAE.

How it works

  1. The application is submitted through the right channel (Work Bundle, Domestic Workers Package or ICP smart services).
  2. The data is reviewed, documents attached and the fees paid.
  3. The residence is issued and the Emirates ID is delivered by courier.

ICP lists the residence service as taking about 2 days after a complete application.

Questions

Employment residence: common questions

How long does an employer have to complete the employment residence after entry?

ICP says the residence must be completed within 60 days of entry, or delay fines apply.

What documents are needed for an employment residence visa?

ICP lists the passport, a photo, a medical report and, depending on the employer, the MOHRE work permit, the employment contract, or the contract with Wage Protection System proof.

How is a domestic worker's residence done?

ICP says domestic workers sponsored by a UAE national or a resident go through the Domestic Workers Package.

Can the residence be done without leaving the UAE?

Yes. ICP says a change of status request inside the country lets the new residence be completed without leaving, after the previous permit is cancelled.
