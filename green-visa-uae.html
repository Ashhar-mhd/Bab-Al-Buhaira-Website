UAE Green Visa: who qualifies for the 5-year residence

The Green Visa lets you live and work in the UAE for 5 years without an employer or family sponsor. Here is who qualifies, according to ICP, and how we help you apply.

Ask on WhatsApp View Golden Visa service

What is the Green Visa?

ICP calls it the Green Residence. It is granted for five years, with the option to renew, and lets the holder live and work in the UAE without a sponsor.

Who qualifies?

Skilled workers, who need all of these:

  • A valid employment contract in the UAE
  • MOHRE professional classification level 1, 2 or 3
  • At least a bachelor's degree
  • A monthly salary of at least AED 15,000

Freelancers and self-employed people, who need:

  • A freelance or self-employment permit from MOHRE
  • At least a bachelor's degree or a specialised diploma
  • Freelance income of at least AED 360,000 in each of the previous two years, or proof of financial solvency for the whole residence period

Investors and business partners, who need proof of investment or partnership in a UAE business, with the required licences and approvals.

Family members

Green Visa holders can sponsor family members, including the spouse and children, according to ICP's requirements. We check the details for your family.

Grace period

ICP says Green Visa holders get a flexible grace period of up to 6 months after the permit expires.

Green Visa or Golden Visa?

The Green Visa is for 5 years. The Golden Visa is a longer residence with different conditions. If you might qualify for both, we tell you which fits you better.

What to send us

  • Passport and current visa copy
  • Degree certificate (attested, if you have it)
  • Employment contract and salary proof, or your freelance permit and income proof

Questions

Green Visa: common questions

What is the UAE Green Visa?

A 5-year residence, renewable, that lets you live and work in the UAE without a sponsor. ICP calls it the Green Residence.

What salary do I need for the Green Visa?

For skilled workers, ICP lists a minimum monthly salary of AED 15,000, plus MOHRE skill level 1 to 3 and at least a bachelor's degree.

Can freelancers get the Green Visa?

Yes, with a MOHRE freelance or self-employment permit, at least a bachelor's degree or specialised diploma, and income of at least AED 360,000 in each of the last two years, or proof of financial solvency.

Can I sponsor my family on a Green Visa?

Yes. ICP says holders can sponsor family members, including the spouse and children, according to its requirements.

What is the grace period for the Green Visa?

ICP says up to 6 months after the permit expires.
