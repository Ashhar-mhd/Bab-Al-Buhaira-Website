Sharjah family visa documents checklist (2026)

Getting your papers ready before you apply is the fastest way to a family visa. Use this checklist for a new family visa or a renewal in Sharjah or Dubai. Rules can change and some cases need extra papers, so we always check your file before we apply.

Ask on WhatsApp All guides

1. Documents from the sponsor

  • Passport copy and current UAE residence visa
  • Emirates ID (front and back)
  • Salary certificate or labour contract
  • Tenancy contract: attested tenancy contract in Sharjah, or Ejari in Dubai
  • A recent electricity and water bill for the same address (SEWA in Sharjah, DEWA in Dubai)

2. For your wife or husband

  • Passport copy, with enough validity left
  • Passport photo with a white background
  • Marriage certificate, attested in the country that issued it, by the UAE embassy there, and by the UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA)
  • Legal Arabic translation of the marriage certificate if it is not in Arabic or English

3. For your children

  • Passport copy and passport photo with a white background
  • Birth certificate showing both parents' names, attested the same way as the marriage certificate

4. For your parents

Parent visas have extra conditions, such as a higher salary and, in some emirates, a security deposit and health insurance. You also need proof that they are your parents, usually your own attested birth certificate. Send us your details first and we tell you whether you qualify.

5. For a renewal

  • Passport copies and the current visa of each family member
  • Emirates ID of each family member
  • Updated sponsor documents (salary, tenancy and utility bill)
  • A new medical fitness test for adult family members, which we book for you

Dubai family visa renewal: from AED 1,175 per adult and AED 875 per child, including government fees and our service charge. Sharjah and new visa prices depend on your case, so ask us for an exact quote.

Tips that save time

  • Start attestation early. Certificates from your home country can take the longest.
  • Make sure names and spellings match across passports and certificates.
  • Send clear, full-page photos of each document on WhatsApp. Cropped or blurry photos slow things down.
