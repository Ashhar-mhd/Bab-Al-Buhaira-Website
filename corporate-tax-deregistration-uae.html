Corporate Tax deregistration in the UAE

When a company closes, is sold or merges, its Corporate Tax registration should be cancelled with the Federal Tax Authority. Here is how the FTA's deregistration service works, and we can prepare it for you.

Ask on WhatsApp View Business setup service

Who can deregister

The FTA service is for anyone registered for Corporate Tax. The FTA lists these reasons for deregistration:

  • Liquidation or bankruptcy
  • Closure of the business
  • Merger, or sale of the business
  • Duplicate tax registration number (TRN)
  • Change of place of effective management, or closure of a permanent establishment
  • Re-domiciliation of the business, or any other reason that requires deregistration

Documents the FTA asks for

  • Closure or liquidation: licence cancellation document, and financial statements up to the licence cancellation date
  • Sale of business: the sale agreement, the licence cancellation or amended licence, and financial statements
  • Merger: the merger agreement, financial statements up to the merger date, and the licence cancellation or amendment
  • Duplicate TRN: the tax registration certificate of the other TRN

Files can be PDF, JPG, PNG or XLSX, up to 15 MB each.

How it works

  1. Check the FTA's eligibility criteria and documents.
  2. Log in to EmaraTax with UAE Pass, using the same account used for Corporate Tax registration.
  3. Fill in and submit the online deregistration form.

How long it takes

The FTA says it processes the application within 40 working days of receiving a complete application. If it asks for more information, it can take up to another 40 working days after you resubmit, and if you do not resubmit within 60 calendar days the application may be rejected. The FTA service itself is free.

What to send us

  • Your trade licence cancellation (or amended licence)
  • Financial statements up to the closing date
  • Your TRN and EmaraTax login details, or we guide you through UAE Pass

Questions

Corporate Tax deregistration: common questions

When should a company deregister from Corporate Tax in the UAE?

The FTA lists reasons such as liquidation, closure of the business, merger, sale of the business, a duplicate TRN, or closure of a permanent establishment.

What documents are needed for Corporate Tax deregistration?

For a business closure, the FTA asks for the licence cancellation document and financial statements up to the cancellation date. Other reasons need other documents, such as a sale or merger agreement.

How long does Corporate Tax deregistration take?

The FTA says it processes a complete application within 40 working days. If it asks for more information, it can take up to another 40 working days after you resubmit.

Is there an FTA fee for Corporate Tax deregistration?

No. The FTA lists the service as free of charge.
