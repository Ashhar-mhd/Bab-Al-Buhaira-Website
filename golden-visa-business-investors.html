Golden Visa for business investors

ICP gives the 10-year Golden Residence to investors in public investments. The key test is AED 2 million of your own money in the UAE. Here is what ICP says, and we can check your case and apply.

ICP's capital tests for investors

ICP's Golden Residency guide lists these ways to qualify as an investor in public investments:

  • A deposit, company capital or partner share in the UAE of at least AED 2,000,000
  • Or a company that pays at least AED 250,000 a year in taxes to the Federal Government

ICP says the invested capital must not come from a loan.

Conditions

  • Valid health insurance when you apply
  • Passport valid for at least 6 months
  • Proof that you meet the Golden Residency conditions
  • ICP's investor visa service is for applicants who are inside the UAE

How it works

  1. We check your proof of investment against ICP's rules.
  2. The application is submitted on the ICP smart services website or app, with UAE Pass.
  3. The data is reviewed, documents attached and the fees paid.
  4. The visa is issued so you can complete the 10-year Golden Residence.

ICP lists the visa service as taking about 2 days after a complete application.

What you get

  • A 10-year Golden Residence that renews, without a sponsor
  • Residence for your spouse, children of any age, and parents

What to send us

  • Passport copy and a recent photo with a white background
  • Trade licence and memorandum, or bank deposit letter, showing your AED 2 million
  • Proof the money is your own (not a loan), and your health insurance

We check your papers first and tell you our service charge before we apply.

Questions

Golden Visa for investors: common questions

How much do I need to invest for a UAE Golden Visa as an investor?

ICP lists a deposit, company capital or partner share in the UAE of at least AED 2,000,000. Another route is a company that pays at least AED 250,000 a year in federal taxes.

Can I use a loan for the Golden Visa investment?

No. ICP says the invested capital for this route must not come from a loan.

Do I need to be in the UAE to apply for the investor Golden Visa?

ICP's investor visa service says the application is made by applicants who are inside the UAE.

Can an investor Golden Visa holder sponsor family?

Yes. ICP says Golden Residence holders can get residence for their spouse, children of any age, and parents.
