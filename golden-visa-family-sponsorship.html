Sponsoring your family on a Golden Visa

A Golden Visa lets you bring your family to the UAE without the usual limits. Here is what ICP's Golden Residency guide says about family members, and what to send us.

Ask on WhatsApp View Golden Visa service

Who a Golden Visa holder can sponsor

  • Spouse
  • Children, regardless of their age. ICP's guide gives no age limit for sons or daughters
  • Parents
  • Domestic workers, under the UAE's rules for domestic workers

This is different from a normal family visa, where there are salary and age rules. See who can I sponsor for the normal rules.

Health insurance for family

For several Golden Visa categories, ICP asks for valid health insurance for the holder and family members, for example investors in public investments, specialised professionals and entrepreneurs. Keep the insurance valid for the residence period.

If the Golden Visa holder dies

ICP says the sponsored family members can stay in the UAE until their own residence permits expire.

Your family's validity

ICP's guide gives the 10-year period and automatic renewal for the holder. It does not state the family members' validity, so we confirm it with ICP when we apply.

What to send us

  • Your Golden Visa and Emirates ID copy
  • Passport copy of each family member
  • A recent photo of each, with a white background, face clearly visible
  • Marriage certificate (for the spouse) and birth certificates (for children), attested where needed. See attestation
  • Health insurance details for each family member

Questions

Golden Visa family sponsorship: common questions

Can a Golden Visa holder sponsor sons over 18?

Yes. ICP's Golden Residency guide says the holder can sponsor children regardless of their age.

Can a Golden Visa holder sponsor parents?

Yes. ICP lists residence permits for parents among the Golden Residency benefits.

What happens to the family if the Golden Visa holder dies?

ICP says sponsored family members can stay in the UAE until their own residence permits expire.

Do Golden Visa family members need health insurance?

For several categories, ICP asks for valid health insurance for the holder and family members for the residence period.

Can a Golden Visa holder sponsor a housemaid?

Yes. ICP says Golden Residency holders can sponsor domestic workers under the UAE's applicable rules.
