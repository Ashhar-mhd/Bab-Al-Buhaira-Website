Lost your passport in the UAE? What to do

Losing your passport is stressful, but there is a clear way to fix it. Here are the answers to the questions we hear most, and we can help you with each step.

Ask on WhatsApp All guides

I lost my passport in the UAE. What should I do first?

Report it to the police and get a lost item certificate (police report). In Sharjah and Dubai you can usually do this online or in the police app. We can help you apply.

How do I get a new passport?

Apply at your country's embassy or consulate in the UAE. For Indian citizens, applications now go through Al Hind. Bring a copy of your old passport and the police report. We can type the form and book the appointment.

I need to travel home urgently. What can I do?

Your embassy can issue an Emergency Certificate, a one-way travel document that lets you return to your home country. It usually needs the police report and immigration clearance. We can guide you through it.

What happens to my UAE visa?

If you have a UAE residence visa, it stays valid. Once you have the new passport, the visa details must be moved to it (passport data update). We can do this for you.

Need help now? Send us a message on WhatsApp with a copy of your old passport, and we tell you the next step.
