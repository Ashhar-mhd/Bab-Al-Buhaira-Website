UAE retirement visa: who qualifies

ICP issues a residence permit for retirees who want to live in the UAE without an employer sponsor. Here are ICP's conditions and documents, and we can prepare the application for you.

Ask on WhatsApp View UAE visas service

First condition: service years or age

ICP says the retiree must have at least 15 years of service before retiring (inside or outside the UAE), or be aged 55 or over.

Second condition: one financial option

  • Property in the UAE worth at least AED 1,000,000, or
  • A deposit of at least AED 1,000,000 in a UAE financial institution, or
  • A fixed annual income of at least AED 240,000, shown with a 6-month bank statement

ICP says the property or deposit should be in place within 60 days of the residence being issued, unless the money is already invested in the UAE.

Documents ICP asks for

  • Passport valid for at least 6 months
  • A recent photo with a white background, face clearly visible
  • Work certificate confirming retirement and years of service, or a birth certificate if you qualify by age
  • Property ownership and valuation certificate, or bank certificate of your fixed annual income and a 6-month bank statement
  • Water and electricity bill
  • Valid health insurance

How it works

  1. The application is submitted on the ICP smart services website or app, with UAE Pass.
  2. The data is reviewed, documents attached and the fees paid.
  3. ICP notifies you when the residence is issued, and the Emirates ID is delivered by courier.

ICP lists the service as taking about 2 days after a complete application. If you are in the UAE on an entry permit, the residence must be completed within 60 days of entry, or overstay fines apply.

Questions

Retirement visa: common questions

Who can get a retirement visa in the UAE?

ICP says a retiree needs at least 15 years of service before retiring, or must be aged 55 or over, plus one of the financial conditions.

What are the financial conditions for the UAE retirement visa?

ICP lists property worth at least AED 1,000,000, a deposit of at least AED 1,000,000 in a UAE financial institution, or a fixed annual income of at least AED 240,000.

Do retirees need health insurance for the UAE residence visa?

Yes. ICP lists valid health insurance as a requirement for the retiree category.

How long does the retiree residence take?

ICP lists the service as taking about 2 days after a complete application.
