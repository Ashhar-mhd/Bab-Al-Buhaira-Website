Trade licence renewal in Sharjah

A Sharjah trade licence must be renewed every year with the Sharjah Economic Development Department (SEDD). Here is what SEDD asks for, when to renew, and how we can do it for you.

Ask on WhatsApp View Business setup service

What SEDD asks for

  • A valid lease contract attested by Sharjah City Municipality, valid for at least 3 months after you submit. See tenancy contract attestation
  • All violations paid. SEDD says a licence cannot be renewed while violations are outstanding
  • A valid establishment card
  • No remarks on the site or the activity
  • Approvals from other authorities, if your activity needs them

When to renew

  • You can renew up to 3 months before the expiry date
  • An expired licence can still be renewed, but SEDD charges a late fee from AED 200 per month, which SEDD says can rise to AED 5,000 or more
  • If a licence stays expired for more than 5 years, SEDD says it is cancelled administratively

Documents

  • Passport and Emirates ID copies of the investors, if required
  • Attested lease contract (and title deed, if required)
  • Approvals from other authorities for your activity, if any

How it works

  1. Log in to SEDD's website or app with UAE Pass and choose "License Renewal". It can also be done at SEDD branches and Tasheel centres
  2. Attach the documents and submit
  3. Pay the payment voucher
  4. The renewed licence is sent automatically. SEDD lists the delivery time as one working day

Questions

Trade licence renewal (Sharjah): common questions

What do I need to renew a trade licence in Sharjah?

SEDD asks for a valid lease contract attested by Sharjah City Municipality, all violations paid, a valid establishment card, and any approvals your activity needs.

How early can I renew my Sharjah trade licence?

SEDD says you can renew up to 3 months before the expiry date.

What is the fine for late trade licence renewal in Sharjah?

SEDD charges a late fee starting from AED 200 per month, which can rise to AED 5,000 or more.

Can I renew my Sharjah licence with unpaid violations?

No. SEDD says the licence cannot be renewed or modified while violations are outstanding. They must be paid first.

How long does Sharjah trade licence renewal take?

SEDD lists the delivery period as one working day.
