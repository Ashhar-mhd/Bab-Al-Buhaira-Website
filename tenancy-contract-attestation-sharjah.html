Tenancy contract attestation in Sharjah

Sharjah City Municipality says a tenancy contract is not binding until the municipality attests it. Here is what you need for a residential contract, and how we can do it for you.

Why attest your contract

Sharjah City Municipality says a tenancy contract is not binding unless the municipality attests it. Attestation protects both the landlord and the tenant. An attested contract is also often asked for in other services, such as a SEWA connection or a family visa.

First: the SEWA step

Before the contract is attested, the municipality asks you to visit SEWA (Sharjah Electricity, Water and Gas Authority). There you pay the electricity deposit, get the meter number written on the contract, and get the contract stamped.

Documents for every contract

  • Title deed copy, or the deed number and date written in the contract
  • Owner's ID copy
  • Tenant's ID copy
  • If an agent signs for the owner: the power of attorney and the agent's ID or passport
  • For granted land: a copy of the land map

Extra documents by type of housing

  • Family housing: passport copy. If the wife's residence is under another sponsor: her ID, her residence and the marriage contract. For Arab houses or villas: a pledge form
  • Single residence: passport copies and valid residence of the people living there
  • Staff accommodation: the company's trade licence, and a company letter with the names of the employees living there

How we do it

  1. You and the landlord agree and sign the contract
  2. You complete the SEWA step (we tell you what to bring)
  3. We submit the contract and documents to Sharjah Municipality
  4. You receive the attested contract

We check your papers first and tell you the total cost before we apply.

Questions

Tenancy contract attestation: common questions

Is tenancy contract attestation required in Sharjah?

Yes. Sharjah City Municipality says a tenancy contract is not binding unless it is attested by the municipality.

What documents are needed to attest a tenancy contract in Sharjah?

The title deed (or deed number and date), the owner's ID and the tenant's ID. Family housing also needs a passport copy, and single residence needs the residents' passports and residence.

Do I need to go to SEWA before tenancy attestation?

Yes. The municipality asks you to pay the electricity deposit at SEWA, get the meter number written on the contract and get it stamped first.

Can a typing centre attest my Sharjah tenancy contract?

Yes. Sharjah Municipality's service can be done through service centres. Send us your contract and documents and we submit it for you.
