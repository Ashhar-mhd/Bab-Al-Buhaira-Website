MOFA attestation in the UAE: how it works now

MOFA attestation is now done online. Some documents get a digital stamp within hours, others go by courier. Here is how it works, and we can submit it for you.

Ask on WhatsApp View Attestation service

What is MOFA attestation?

MOFA (the UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs) attestation is the final stamp that makes a document officially accepted by UAE authorities, for example a degree, marriage or birth certificate, or a company document. It is often the last step after the document has been attested by the authority that issued it.

How to apply

Applications are made online on the MOFA website or the UAE MOFA app, using UAE Pass to log in. Payment is made online. If you don't have UAE Pass or prefer not to do it yourself, we apply for you.

Two ways your document is attested

  • Digital attestation: for digital documents issued by a UAE government entity that have a QR code, barcode, link or reference number. You upload the original PDF, and MOFA adds an electronic stamp on the last page. MOFA says this is done within about 2 hours during working hours. The attested PDF comes by email or from "My Requests".
  • Courier route: for paper documents that don't qualify for the digital route. A courier collects the original and returns it attested, usually in 1 to 3 working days inside the UAE.

Before you apply

  • The original must already be attested or certified by the authority that issued it.
  • It must be in Arabic or English. Documents in other languages need a legal translation.
  • The document must not be heat-laminated.
  • Commercial invoices and certificates of origin use a separate MOFA system (eDAS).

Documents issued outside the UAE

A degree or certificate from another country usually needs attestation in that country first, then by the UAE embassy there, before MOFA in the UAE. The exact chain depends on the country, so send us a photo and we tell you what your document needs.

How to check an attestation is real

MOFA attestations can be verified on verify.mofa.gov.ae using the reference number on the stamp.

MOFA fees depend on the document type, personal or commercial. We tell you the exact total before you pay, with the government fee and our service charge listed separately.

Questions

MOFA attestation: common questions

How do I apply for MOFA attestation?

Online on the MOFA website or the UAE MOFA app, logging in with UAE Pass and paying online. If you don't have UAE Pass, we can apply for you.

How long does MOFA attestation take?

MOFA says digital attestation is done within about 2 hours in working hours. Paper documents sent by courier usually take 1 to 3 working days inside the UAE.

What is MOFA digital attestation?

For digital documents issued by a UAE government entity with a QR code or reference number, MOFA adds an electronic stamp to the PDF. No paper is sent.

Does my document need translation before MOFA attestation?

MOFA accepts documents in Arabic or English. Documents in other languages need a legal translation. We do legal translation in Arabic, English, Malayalam, Hindi, Urdu and Tamil.

How can I check if a MOFA attestation is genuine?

Go to verify.mofa.gov.ae and enter the reference number shown on the attestation stamp.
