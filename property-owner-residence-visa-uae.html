UAE visas · Property owners · ICP

Residence visa for property owners

ICP has a residence permit category for people who own property in the UAE. It is separate from the Golden Visa for property investors. Here are ICP's conditions, and we can apply for you.

Ask on WhatsApp View UAE visas service

ICP's property conditions

ICP says the property must be:

  • Fully constructed (completed, not off-plan)
  • Registered with the real estate registration authority
  • Fully owned by the applicant
  • Suitable for living in

Income condition

ICP asks for a monthly income of at least AED 10,000, or proof that you can support yourself for the whole time you stay in the UAE.

Documents ICP asks for

  • Passport valid for at least 6 months
  • A recent photo with a white background, face clearly visible
  • Water and electricity bill for the property

Keep your title deed and proof of income ready as well, so we can check the conditions before applying.

How it works

  1. The application is submitted on the ICP smart services website or app, with UAE Pass.
  2. The data is reviewed, documents attached and the fees paid.
  3. ICP notifies you when the residence is issued, and the Emirates ID is delivered by courier.

ICP lists the service as taking about 2 days after a complete application. If you are in the UAE on an entry permit, finish within 60 days of entry to avoid overstay fines.

Property owner visa or Golden Visa?

If your property is worth AED 2 million or more, you may qualify for the 10-year Golden Visa for property owners instead. Send us your title deed and we tell you which one fits.

Questions

Property owner visa: common questions

Can I get a UAE residence visa by owning property?

Yes. ICP has a Property Owner residence category. The property must be fully constructed, registered, fully owned by you and suitable for living in.

What income do I need for the property owner visa?

ICP asks for a monthly income of at least AED 10,000, or proof that you can support yourself for your whole stay.

Can I use an off-plan property for the property owner residence?

No. ICP says the property must be fully constructed. Off-plan units can count for the Golden Visa route instead.

Is the property owner visa the same as the Golden Visa?

No. It is a separate ICP category. Property worth AED 2 million or more may qualify for the 10-year Golden Visa.
