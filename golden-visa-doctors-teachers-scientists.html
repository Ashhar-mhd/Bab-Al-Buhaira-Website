Golden Visa · Specialists · ICP

Golden Visa for doctors, teachers and scientists

ICP gives a 10-year Golden Residence to leading scientists and specialists. For most of these groups, the key step is a recommendation from the right UAE ministry or authority. Here is which one you need.

Ask on WhatsApp View Golden Visa service

Who recommends you

ICP's Golden Residency guide lists these groups, each with the body that recommends them:

  • Healthcare specialists (leading doctors and medical specialists): Ministry of Health and Prevention, or the relevant local authority
  • Education specialists (leading teachers and educators): Ministry of Education, or the relevant local authority
  • Scientists and researchers with significant achievements: Emirates Scientists Council
  • Industry and Fourth Industrial Revolution specialists: Ministry of Industry and Advanced Technology, or the relevant local authority
  • Leading scientists and religious scholars: Ministry of Culture and Youth, or the relevant local authority

If you are a senior employee with a high salary rather than a recommended specialist, see our Golden Visa for skilled professionals guide instead.

Health insurance

ICP says the applicant and family must keep comprehensive, valid health insurance for the whole residence period.

What you get

  • A 10-year residence with automatic renewal, without a sponsor
  • Residence for your spouse, children of any age, and parents
  • You can sponsor domestic workers
  • If you are outside the UAE, a six-month multiple-entry visa (renewable once) to finish the procedures

What to send us

  • Passport copy and a recent photo with a white background
  • Your degree, licence and proof of your achievements or position
  • The recommendation letter, if you already have it

Questions

Golden Visa for specialists: common questions

Can doctors get the UAE Golden Visa?

Yes. ICP lists leading healthcare specialists, with a recommendation from the Ministry of Health and Prevention or the relevant local authority.

Can teachers get the UAE Golden Visa?

ICP lists leading education specialists, with a recommendation from the Ministry of Education or the relevant local authority.

Who recommends scientists for the Golden Visa?

ICP says scientists and researchers with significant achievements need a recommendation from the Emirates Scientists Council.

Is health insurance needed for the specialist Golden Visa?

Yes. ICP says the applicant and family must keep valid health insurance for the whole residence period.
