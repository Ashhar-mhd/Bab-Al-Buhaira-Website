Emirates ID fine waiver: can you get the delay fine removed?

If your Emirates ID expired and was not renewed in time, ICP charges a delay fine. In some cases you can ask ICP to exempt you from it. Here is how it works, and we can submit the request with your renewal.

Ask on WhatsApp View Emirates ID service

The Emirates ID delay fine

ICP charges a fine of AED 20 for each day of delay in renewing the Emirates ID, up to a maximum of AED 1,000. A separate fine applies if you do not report a change in your personal data within 30 days.

ICP's exemption service

ICP has a service called "Request for Exemption from Delay Fees". It asks for an exemption from the delay fees linked to the ID card, based on ICP's approved exemption decisions. The request is made together with your ID card application (new card or renewal).

Cases ICP lists

  • The ID expired after deportation, or while the passport was held because of a court case, with proof from the authorities
  • The ID expired while a passport reservation was in place because of a legal case, with proof from the competent authority
  • The person did not receive an ID before getting nationality and family data, which caused the delay

ICP decides each request. It may accept, reject, or ask for changes. Other reasons are not listed on ICP's page, so we check your case honestly before applying and tell you if it is unlikely to be accepted.

How it works

  1. We start your Emirates ID renewal or new card application.
  2. We submit the exemption request with it and attach your proof.
  3. ICP reviews it. ICP lists the service as taking about 2 days.

What to send us

  • Passport copy with UAE visa, and your Emirates ID number
  • Proof for your case, for example a court or police letter
  • A recent photo with a white background, face clearly visible

We tell you the exact total before you pay, with the government fee and our service charge listed separately.

Emirates ID fine waiver: common questions

How much is the fine for late Emirates ID renewal?

ICP charges AED 20 for each day of delay, up to a maximum of AED 1,000.

Can I get my Emirates ID delay fine waived?

In some cases. ICP has a Request for Exemption from Delay Fees service. It lists cases such as an ID that expired after deportation or while a passport was held in a court case, with proof from the authorities.

When do I apply for the Emirates ID fine exemption?

ICP says the exemption request is submitted together with the ID card application, for a new card or a renewal.

How long does ICP take to decide on a fine exemption?

ICP lists the service as taking about 2 days. It can accept, reject or ask for changes.
