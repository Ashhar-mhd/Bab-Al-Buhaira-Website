VAT registration in the UAE: who must register

If your business sales pass a set amount, you must register for VAT with the Federal Tax Authority (FTA). Here is when you need to register, what to prepare, and how we can help.

Ask on WhatsApp View Business setup service

Do you need to register for VAT?

  • Must register (mandatory): your taxable sales and imports in the last 12 months were more than AED 375,000, or you expect them to pass that in the next 30 days.
  • Can register (voluntary): your taxable sales and imports, or your taxable expenses, in the last 12 months were more than AED 187,500, or you expect them to pass that in the next 30 days.

These amounts are for businesses based in the UAE. Foreign businesses follow different rules.

Deadline

Once you must register, you have 30 days to apply to the FTA. If you apply late, the FTA charges a late registration penalty.

How it works

  1. Create an account on EmaraTax, the FTA's online platform. You sign in with UAE Pass.
  2. Create a taxable person profile for your business.
  3. Apply for VAT registration and upload the documents.
  4. The FTA reviews it. It says this takes up to 20 business days once the application is complete. When approved, you get a Tax Registration Number (TRN).

The FTA does not charge a fee for VAT registration.

Documents usually needed

  • Valid trade licence (and branch licences, if any)
  • Emirates ID and passport copies of the owners and authorised signatories
  • Memorandum of association or partnership agreement, if you have one
  • Declaration letter with your total taxable sales and monthly sales
  • Bank letter with the company's account details (optional)

The FTA list changes with the type of company, and files must be PDF. We check what you need before we start.

VAT registration is free with the FTA. Our service charge covers preparing and submitting the application, and we tell you the amount before we start.

Questions

VAT registration: common questions

When must a business register for VAT in the UAE?

When its taxable sales and imports in the last 12 months were more than AED 375,000, or are expected to pass that in the next 30 days. This is the FTA's mandatory threshold for UAE businesses.

Can a small business register for VAT voluntarily?

Yes. If taxable sales and imports, or taxable expenses, were more than AED 187,500 in the last 12 months, or are expected to pass that in the next 30 days, the business can register voluntarily.

How long do I have to apply for VAT registration?

You must apply within 30 days of being required to register. The FTA charges a penalty for late registration.

Is there a government fee for VAT registration?

No. The FTA lists VAT registration as free. It is done online on EmaraTax with UAE Pass.
