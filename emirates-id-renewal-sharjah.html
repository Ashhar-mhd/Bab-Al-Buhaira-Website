Emirates ID renewal in Sharjah

In Sharjah your Emirates ID is renewed through ICP, normally together with your residence visa. Here is what you need, how long it takes and how to avoid late fines. We can type the whole renewal for you.

How Emirates ID renewal works in Sharjah

For residents of Sharjah and the other emirates outside Dubai, the Emirates ID is handled by ICP (Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs and Port Security). When your residence visa is renewed, the Emirates ID is renewed in the same application, so you get one new card that matches your new visa.

When to start

Start about 30 days before your Emirates ID or visa expires. This gives time for the medical test, any biometrics appointment and delivery of the new card. You can check how many days you have left with the expiry checker on our home page.

Documents you need

  • Passport with at least 3 months validity
  • A recent passport photo on a white background (it must be different from the photo on your current card)
  • Your current Emirates ID
  • Medical fitness result (needed when the residence visa is renewed)

Your sponsor or company may need to give extra documents depending on the visa type. Send us your documents on WhatsApp and we tell you exactly what is missing.

The steps

  1. We check your documents and type the renewal application.
  2. You do the medical fitness test if your visa is being renewed.
  3. If ICP asks for it, you go to an ICP centre for fingerprints. This applies to people aged 15 and above.
  4. Once approved, the new card is delivered to you by ICP's courier.

Late renewal fine

If you renew more than one month after your Emirates ID expires, there is a late fine of AED 20 per day, up to a maximum of AED 1,000. ICP has a service to ask for a fine waiver in some cases, and we can apply for it for you.

How to check your renewal status

Use the ICP card status page (icp.gov.ae) and enter your application number or Emirates ID number. You can also ask us on WhatsApp and we check it for you.

Government fees depend on how many years of residence the card covers. We tell you the exact total before you pay, with the government fee and our service charge listed separately.

Questions

Emirates ID renewal: common questions

When should I renew my Emirates ID in Sharjah?

Start about 30 days before it expires. In Sharjah the Emirates ID is normally renewed together with your residence visa, in one application through ICP.

Is there a fine for renewing my Emirates ID late?

Yes. If you renew more than one month after expiry, the fine is AED 20 per day, up to AED 1,000. In some cases you can ask ICP for a fine waiver, and we can apply for it for you.

Do I need to give fingerprints again to renew my Emirates ID?

Only if ICP asks for it. Biometrics (fingerprints) apply to people aged 15 and above. We tell you if you need to go to an ICP centre.

How long does Emirates ID renewal take?

ICP's standard time is about 5 working days after approval, and the new card is delivered by courier. The medical test and any biometrics appointment can add a few days, so start early.

How do I check my Emirates ID renewal status?

On the ICP website (icp.gov.ae), use the card status page with your application number or Emirates ID number. Or send us a WhatsApp message and we check it for you.
