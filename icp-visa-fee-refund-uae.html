ICP fee refund: getting your visa fees back

If you paid ICP for a service that was not completed, you can ask ICP for a refund. Here is what ICP refunds, what you need, and how we can apply for you.

Ask on WhatsApp View UAE visas service

ICP's Refund of Fees service

ICP describes this as a service to request a refund of issuance fees when the service is not completed. It covers ICP services such as visas, residence and Emirates ID.

Which fees come back

  • Issuance fees can be refunded.
  • Application submission fees and smart services fees are not refunded.
  • The fees that can be refunded are shown on your service receipt, so keep it.

What you need

  • A certificate from your bank showing your account number and IBAN
  • The application or receipt number of the service you paid for

How it works

  1. The refund request is submitted on the ICP smart services website or app, with UAE Pass.
  2. The data is reviewed and the bank certificate is attached.
  3. ICP accepts or rejects the request.
  4. If accepted, the money is transferred to your bank account.

ICP lists the service as taking about 5 days.

Approved applications are different

ICP has a separate service, "Refund of fees for approved applications", for getting back a financial guarantee (deposit) after an approved application. Tell us which case you have and we use the right one.

What to send us

  • Your ICP receipt or application number
  • Bank certificate with your IBAN
  • Passport and Emirates ID copy

We tell you our service charge before we apply. The refund itself goes straight from ICP to your bank account.

Last checked 17 November 2026 against ICP (icp.gov.ae, Refund of Fees). Government rules can change, so we confirm everything before you pay.

Questions

ICP fee refund: common questions

Can I get my ICP visa fee back if the visa was not completed?

ICP has a Refund of Fees service for issuance fees when the service is not completed. Application submission fees and smart services fees are not refunded.

What documents do I need for an ICP refund?

ICP asks for a certificate from your bank showing your account number and IBAN. Keep your service receipt, because it shows which fees can be refunded.

How long does an ICP refund take?

ICP lists the service as taking about 5 days. Once accepted, the money is transferred to your bank account.

Can a typing centre apply for my ICP refund?

Yes. Send us your receipt and bank IBAN certificate and we submit the refund request for you.
