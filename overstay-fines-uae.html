Overstay fines in the UAE: how to check and clear them

Stayed past your visit visa or residence visa? A daily fine adds up until it is paid. Here is how overstay fines work, how to check yours, and how we help you clear it.

Ask on WhatsApp View UAE visas service

How much is the overstay fine?

ICP lists a fine of AED 50 per day when a foreigner's permitted stay ends, or when an entry visa or residence permit expires or is cancelled and the person stays on. This covers both visitors and residents.

When does the fine start?

  • Visit visa: after the permitted stay on the visa ends.
  • Residence visa: after it expires or is cancelled and any grace period has ended.

The grace period depends on the visa type, so we check your case before anything else.

How to check your fine

  • ICP Smart Services (website or app), logging in with UAE Pass, for visas issued outside Dubai, such as Sharjah.
  • GDRFA Dubai fines inquiry for Dubai visas, using the file number, Emirates ID or passport number.
  • Or send us your passport and visa copy on WhatsApp and we check it for you.

Paying the fine and what comes next

ICP's terms say the person must pay the fines and then either adjust their status or leave the UAE. In practice that means one of these:

  • Leaving the UAE: pay the fine first, then travel.
  • Staying legally: pay the fine and change to a new visa, for example a new visit visa, an employment visa or a family visa, if you qualify.

What to send us

  • Passport copy with the last UAE visa
  • Emirates ID copy, if you had one
  • Your plan: leave the UAE or apply for a new visa

Overstay fines are set by the government, AED 50 per day. We tell you the exact total before you pay, with the fine and our service charge listed separately.

Last checked 9 November 2026 against ICP (icp.gov.ae), GDRFA Dubai (gdrfad.gov.ae) and u.ae. Government rules can change, so we confirm everything before you pay.

Questions

Overstay fines: common questions

How much is the overstay fine in the UAE?

ICP lists AED 50 per day after the permitted stay ends, or after a visa or residence permit expires or is cancelled.

How can I check my overstay fine?

On ICP Smart Services with UAE Pass for visas outside Dubai, or the GDRFA Dubai fines inquiry for Dubai visas. You can also send us your passport and visa copy and we check it.

Do I have to pay the overstay fine before leaving the UAE?

Yes. ICP says fines must be paid before the person changes status or leaves the UAE.

Can I change to a new visa instead of leaving?

Often yes, after the fine is paid, if you qualify for a new visit, employment or family visa. We check your case and apply.
