Emirates ID for your children and newborn

Every resident in your family needs an Emirates ID, including children. Here is how it works for kids and newborns according to ICP, and we can apply for the whole family.

Ask on WhatsApp View Emirates ID service

Who applies for a child's Emirates ID?

ICP says the application can be made by the head of the family, a legal representative or guardian, or by the person themselves once they reach the legal age. For residents, the sponsor applies for the family members, because the Emirates ID is linked to the residence visa on one combined application. In every emirate except Dubai, it is submitted together with the residence application.

Fingerprints: from age 15

ICP says anyone who has completed 15 years of age must give fingerprints and a signature at an ICP service centre. If your child is 15 or older, we tell you when and where to go.

Newborn babies: don't miss the 4 months

For a newborn of a resident family, ICP starts counting a delay fine four months after the date of birth. The fine is AED 20 for each day of delay, up to AED 1,000. Apply for your baby's residence and Emirates ID before then.

Delivery and urgent service

  • Normal service: the card is delivered by ICP's courier. ICP lists the service as taking 5 days.
  • Urgent service: you collect the card from an ICP centre, and you need to visit the centre to apply.

What to send us

  • Your child's passport (valid for at least 3 months)
  • A recent photo of your child with a white background, face clearly visible
  • Birth certificate, attested if issued outside the UAE
  • Your passport, UAE visa and Emirates ID as the sponsor

If your family details change, such as a new passport, ICP expects the change to be reported within 30 days. We can update the data for you.

We tell you the exact total before you pay, with the government fee and our service charge listed separately.

Questions

Emirates ID for children: common questions

Does my child need an Emirates ID?

Yes. Every resident family member gets an Emirates ID with their residence visa. The sponsor applies for the children on the combined residence and ID application.

At what age do children give fingerprints for the Emirates ID?

ICP says anyone who has completed 15 years of age must give fingerprints and a signature at an ICP service centre.

Is there a fine if I register my newborn late?

Yes. For a newborn of a resident family, ICP counts a delay fine from four months after the birth: AED 20 per day, up to AED 1,000.

How is my child's Emirates ID delivered?

With the normal service, ICP's courier delivers the card. With the urgent service, you collect it from an ICP centre.
