UAE Blue Residence: who qualifies

Blue Residence is a long-term UAE residence launched in 2024 for people who make exceptional contributions to environmental protection and sustainability, inside or outside the UAE. Here is what ICP requires.

ICP's 5 Blue Residence categories

  • Environmental and sustainability influencers: proof of financial support of at least AED 1 million, with accreditation and reports on how the funds were used.
  • Scientists and researchers: a nomination from the UAE Council of Scientists, and a master's or PhD in an environmental or energy field.
  • Investors and entrepreneurs: fully owned capital of at least AED 2 million, registration as an SME, annual revenue of at least AED 1 million, and approval from the relevant authorities.
  • Inventors and innovators: a patent in the environmental field.
  • Elite specialists in environmental organisations: a nomination from the relevant authorities and at least a bachelor's degree.

Basic documents for everyone

  • Passport valid for more than 6 months
  • A recent photo with a white background, face clearly visible
  • Valid health insurance
  • Proof of medical fitness

What you get

  • A 10-year renewable residence, without a sponsor in the UAE
  • Residence for your spouse, children of any age, and parents, and you can sponsor domestic workers
  • A six-month multiple-entry visa, renewable once, to finish the procedures from abroad

How to apply

ICP lists two ways: you apply yourself online, or you are nominated by the relevant authority. To keep the residence, you must keep meeting the requirements and keep valid health insurance. ICP also says a Golden Residence can be converted to Blue Residence if you meet the requirements.

Questions

Blue Residence: common questions

What is the UAE Blue Residence?

It is a 10-year renewable UAE residence, launched in 2024, for people who make exceptional contributions to environmental protection and sustainability.

Who qualifies for the Blue Residence?

ICP lists 5 categories: environmental influencers, scientists and researchers, investors and entrepreneurs, inventors and innovators, and elite specialists in environmental organisations.

Can Blue Residence holders sponsor family?

Yes. ICP says holders can get residence for their spouse, children of any age, and parents, and can sponsor domestic workers.

Can I change my Golden Visa to a Blue Residence?

ICP says a Golden Residence can be converted to Blue Residence if you meet the Blue Residence requirements.
