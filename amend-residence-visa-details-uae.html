Change or correct details on your residence visa

If the details on your residence permit are wrong or have changed, ICP has a service to amend them. Here is how it works, and we can submit it for you.

Ask on WhatsApp View UAE visas service

ICP's Amendment of Residency Permit Data

ICP describes this as the service through which residence permit data is modified. A common case is a change of profession on an employee's residence.

Who can apply

  • The resident
  • The employer (a government or private entity, or a free zone establishment) for its employee
  • An accredited UAE educational institution for a foreign student

Documents ICP asks for

  • Passport
  • Personal photo: a recent photo with a white background, face clearly visible
  • Work card, if you are changing your profession

How it works

  1. The application is submitted on the ICP smart services website or app, with UAE Pass.
  2. The data is reviewed and corrected, and documents are attached if needed.
  3. The fees are paid.
  4. ICP sends a notification when the amendment is approved or rejected.

ICP lists the service as taking about 2 days. If the procedure is not completed in time, the application can be cancelled, so it is best to send everything at once.

Your Emirates ID

When your details change, the Emirates ID card is replaced to show the new information. The new card keeps the same validity as your current card.

What to send us

  • Passport copy and Emirates ID copy
  • Photo with a white background
  • What needs to change, with proof (for example the new work card)

If your visa was issued in Dubai, ask us first and we check which authority handles your case.

We tell you the exact total before you pay, with the government fee and our service charge listed separately.

Last checked 15 November 2026 against ICP (icp.gov.ae, Amendment of Residency Permit Data). Government rules can change, so we confirm everything before you pay.

Questions

Amend residence visa details: common questions

How do I change my profession on my UAE residence visa?

ICP has an Amendment of Residency Permit Data service. For a profession change, the work card is needed along with the passport and photo. We can submit it for you.

How long does it take to amend residence visa details?

ICP lists the service as taking about 2 days after a complete application.

Do I get a new Emirates ID after changing my visa details?

Yes. The card is replaced to show the new information. The new card keeps the same validity.

Who can apply to amend residence permit data?

The resident, the employer for an employee, or an accredited educational institution for a foreign student.
