UAE student visa: rules and documents

Foreign students at accredited UAE universities and colleges get a residence permit in the student category. Here is what ICP requires and how long the visa lasts.

Ask on WhatsApp View UAE visas service

Who applies

ICP lists the student category under educational institutions in the UAE accredited for foreign students. In practice, your university or college sponsors the visa and submits the application.

How long it lasts

  • The residence period is set by your study program.
  • ICP says a student can stay 180 days after the study period ends.

Documents ICP asks for

  • Passport valid for at least 6 months
  • A recent photo with a white background, face clearly visible
  • Approval certificate from the Ministry of Education or the emirate's education authority
  • University enrollment certificate
  • Acceptance letter showing the program and its duration
  • Valid health insurance

How it works

  1. The institution submits the application on the ICP smart services website or app.
  2. The data is reviewed, documents attached and the fees paid.
  3. The residence is issued and the Emirates ID is delivered by courier. Students aged 15 or over give fingerprints at an ICP centre.

ICP lists the service as taking about 2 days after a complete application. If you entered on an entry permit, the residence must be finished within 60 days of entry, or overstay fines apply.

Who sponsors a student visa in the UAE?

ICP lists the student category under UAE educational institutions accredited for foreign students, so the university or college applies for the student.

How long is a UAE student visa valid?

ICP says the duration is set by the study program, and the student can stay 180 days after the study period ends.

What documents are needed for a UAE student visa?

ICP lists the passport, a photo, the Ministry of Education or emirate authority approval, the enrollment certificate, the acceptance letter, and valid health insurance.

Can I stay in the UAE after finishing my studies?

ICP says a student can stay 180 days after the study period ends.
