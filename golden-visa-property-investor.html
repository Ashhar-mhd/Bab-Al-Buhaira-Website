Golden Visa for property owners in the UAE

If you own property in the UAE worth at least AED 2 million, you may qualify for the Golden Visa as a real estate investor. Here are ICP's current rules, and we can check your case and apply for you.

Ask on WhatsApp View Golden Visa service

Who qualifies as a property investor

ICP's Golden Residency Guide gives two ways to qualify through real estate:

  • Ready property: you own one or more properties in the UAE with a total value of at least AED 2,000,000, fully registered in your name.
  • Off-plan property: you bought one or more off-plan units with a total value of at least AED 2,000,000 from an approved local real estate company.

Can the property be on a bank loan?

Yes. ICP's guide says the property may be financed through a loan, as long as the loan is from an approved local bank.

How long is it valid?

ICP's guide lists 10 years for real estate investors, renewable, with no sponsor needed. Some older pages still say 5 years, so we confirm the period for your case before you apply.

Your family

  • You can get residence for your spouse and children, whatever their age, and for your parents.
  • You can sponsor domestic workers under the normal rules.
  • You and your family need valid health insurance for the whole period of the residence.

Applying from outside the UAE

If you are outside the UAE, ICP can issue a six-month multiple-entry visa, renewable once for six more months, so you can come and finish the Golden Residency steps.

What to send us

  • Passport copy (and UAE visa and Emirates ID, if you have them)
  • Title deed, or the off-plan purchase papers
  • Bank letter, if the property is on a loan
  • A recent photo with a white background, face clearly visible

We check your papers against ICP's rules first and tell you if anything is missing.

Questions

Golden Visa for property: common questions

What property value do I need for the UAE Golden Visa?

ICP's guide asks for one or more UAE properties worth at least AED 2,000,000 in total, fully registered in your name, or off-plan units worth at least AED 2,000,000 from an approved developer.

Can I get the Golden Visa if my property is on a mortgage?

Yes. ICP's guide says the property may be financed through a loan from an approved local bank.

Can I get the Golden Visa with an off-plan property?

Yes, if you bought off-plan units worth at least AED 2,000,000 in total from an approved local real estate company.

Can a Golden Visa holder sponsor parents?

Yes. ICP's guide says Golden Residency holders can get residence permits for their spouse, children of any age and parents.
