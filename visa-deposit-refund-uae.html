Getting your visa deposit back

Some UAE visas need a financial guarantee (deposit). Once the visa has been used properly, the deposit can be refunded through ICP. Here is how it works.

Ask on WhatsApp View Visit visa service

ICP's Refund of fees for approved applications

ICP describes this as the service that refunds financial guarantees collected for approved applications. ICP says it applies to all categories.

When a refund is blocked

ICP says that if the guarantee is linked to a circular (for example an absconding or similar case), the circular must be lifted first. Only then can the refund be requested.

How it works

  1. The refund request is submitted on the ICP smart services website, with UAE Pass.
  2. The details are reviewed and corrected if needed.
  3. Documents are attached only if the system cannot fetch the data.
  4. ICP processes the refund.

ICP lists the service as taking about 2 days.

Deposit refund vs fee refund

  • Deposit (guarantee) refund: for an approved application where you paid a guarantee. This page.
  • Fee refund: when a service was not completed and you want the issuance fee back. See our ICP fee refund guide.

What to send us

  • The visa or application number and the deposit receipt
  • Sponsor's passport and Emirates ID copy
  • Proof that the visitor left the UAE or changed status, if you have it

We tell you our service charge before we apply. The deposit itself is refunded by ICP.

Last checked 17 November 2026 against ICP (icp.gov.ae, Refund of fees for approved applications). Government rules can change, so we confirm everything before you pay.

Questions

Visa deposit refund: common questions

How do I get my UAE visa deposit back?

ICP has a service called Refund of fees for approved applications, which refunds financial guarantees. It is requested on the ICP smart services website with UAE Pass, and we can do it for you.

Why can't I get my visa deposit refunded?

ICP says that if the guarantee is linked to a circular, such as an absconding case, the circular must be lifted before the refund can be requested.

How long does a visa deposit refund take?

ICP lists the service as taking about 2 days.

What is the difference between a deposit refund and a fee refund?

A deposit refund returns a guarantee paid for an approved application. A fee refund returns the issuance fee when a service was not completed.
