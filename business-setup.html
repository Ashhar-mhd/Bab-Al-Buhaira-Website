Frequently asked questions

How do I start a business in Sharjah?

Choose your business activity and legal form, reserve a trade name, get initial approval, arrange an office or shop, and then issue the licence. We guide you through each step.

Can you renew my existing trade licence?

Yes. Send us your current licence and we handle the renewal and any required approvals.

Do you handle employee visas for my company?

Yes. We process work permits, entry permits, medical, Emirates ID and residence stamping for your staff.

Do you offer monthly PRO services?

Yes. Many companies use us for their regular government work. Ask us on WhatsApp about ongoing PRO support.
