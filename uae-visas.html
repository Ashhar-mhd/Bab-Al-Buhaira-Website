Questions

Frequently asked questions

Which authority processes my visa?

It depends on the emirate of the visa. Sharjah and the other emirates use ICP, and Dubai uses GDRFA. We apply through the correct one for you.

Can you cancel a visa?

Yes. We handle residence visa cancellation. Send us the passport and visa copy on WhatsApp and we tell you the steps.

Do you handle newborn baby visas?

Yes. We prepare the newborn visa application. Send us the birth certificate and the parents' documents and we guide you through it.
