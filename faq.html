    General

    Do I need to visit the office?

    Not always. Many services start on WhatsApp with photos of your documents. Medical tests and Emirates ID biometrics need you to attend in person at the government centre, and we tell you where and when.

    Are the calculator prices final?

    They are our current prices, including government fees and our service charge. Government fees can change without notice, so we confirm your final price before you pay anything.

    Do you handle visas for other emirates?

    Yes. We process applications for Sharjah, Dubai and the other emirates through the correct authority for each, such as ICP or GDRFA.

    Which languages can you translate?

    We arrange legal translation in more than 100 languages, including Arabic, English, Malayalam, Hindi, Urdu and Tamil. We also attest documents issued in over 100 countries.

    Are you a government office?

    No. Bab Al Buhaira is a private typing and services centre. We prepare and submit applications to government offices on your behalf.

    UAE visas

    Which authority processes my visa?

    It depends on the emirate of the visa. Sharjah and the other emirates use ICP, and Dubai uses GDRFA. We apply through the correct one for you.

    Can you cancel a visa?

    Yes. We handle residence visa cancellation. Send us the passport and visa copy on WhatsApp and we tell you the steps.

    Do you handle newborn baby visas?

    Yes. We prepare the newborn visa application. Send us the birth certificate and the parents' documents and we guide you through it.

    How do I know if I have an absconding case?

    Call the MOHRE contact centre on 600 590000, or send us your passport copy and Emirates ID on WhatsApp and we check with MOHRE for you.

    When can my employer file an absconding case?

    Only after you have been absent without a valid reason for more than 7 days in a row, and the employer cannot reach you or does not know where you are.

    Can an absconding case be removed?

    Yes, in some cases. MOHRE can cancel it if you were absent less than 7 days, were really at work or on leave, the employer agrees to take you back, the absence was agreed, or there was a valid reason. We can help you file the request.

    What happens if an absconding case is proven?

    You get a one-year ban on a new work permit. It starts when you leave the UAE and ends on its own. Some people, like those on a family member's visa or Golden Visa holders, are not affected.

    Who cancels my residence visa?

    For employees, the employer cancels it. For family members, the sponsor (husband, wife or parent) applies. We can do the typing and submission for you.

    How long can I stay after my visa is cancelled?

    There is a grace period to leave the UAE or change to a new visa. Its length depends on your visa type, so send us your details and we confirm it for you.

    What is the fine if I stay after the grace period?

    ICP charges an overstay fine of AED 50 per day after the grace period ends.

    Can I change to a new visa without leaving the UAE?

    Usually yes, with a status change inside the UAE. We check your case and apply for it.

    My employer is not cancelling my visa, or the company is closed. What can I do?

    You can register a labour complaint with MOHRE. The labour cancellation is done through the complaint. After that, the immigration (residence visa) cancellation is a separate application. We can handle both for you.

    Family visa

    How long does a family visa renewal take?

    Most renewals are completed within a few working days once the medical test and biometrics are done. Timing depends on the authority and on any extra checks, and we tell you the expected timeline when we start.

    Can I sponsor my parents?

    Yes, in many cases. Parent sponsorship has extra conditions, such as a higher salary and in some emirates a security deposit and health insurance. Send us your details and we confirm whether you qualify.

    Do my documents need attestation?

    Marriage and birth certificates issued outside the UAE normally need attestation in the home country and by the UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs. We can arrange this for you as well.

    Can you process Dubai family visas from Sharjah?

    Yes. We apply through the correct authority for each emirate, including GDRFA for Dubai and ICP for Sharjah and the other emirates.

    Emirates ID & medical

    When should I renew my Emirates ID?

    We suggest starting about 30 days before it expires. The Emirates ID is normally renewed together with the residence visa.

    Do I need to go for biometrics?

    Many applicants need to give fingerprints and a photo at an ICP centre. We tell you if this applies to you and where to go.

    What if I lost my Emirates ID?

    We can apply for a replacement card. In some cases a police report is needed first, and we guide you through it.

    Can you book my medical test?

    Yes. We type the medical fitness application and tell you the centre and timing.

    Can I change the mobile number registered on my Emirates ID?

    Yes. We can change the mobile number linked to your Emirates ID. You confirm the request with your UAE Pass, and you get a one-time code (OTP) on the new number. No UAE Pass? We can still help, see below. Send us a copy of your Emirates ID and the new number on WhatsApp and we take care of the rest.

    Can I change the email address on my Emirates ID?

    Yes. We can change the email address at the same time as the mobile number, or on its own. As with the mobile number, you confirm the request with your UAE Pass.

    What if I don't have UAE Pass?

    We can still help. Send us a copy of your Emirates ID on WhatsApp, and we check your case and let you know how we can update your mobile number or email.

    I lost my old SIM and can't receive the OTP. What can I do?

    That is not a problem. The one-time code (OTP) is sent to your new number, not the old one. You confirm the request with your UAE Pass and we update your Emirates ID with the new number. Send us your Emirates ID copy and your new number on WhatsApp.

    Is there a fine for renewing my Emirates ID late?

    Yes. If you renew more than one month after expiry, the fine is AED 20 per day, up to AED 1,000. In some cases you can ask ICP for a fine waiver, and we can apply for it for you.

    Do I need to give fingerprints again to renew my Emirates ID?

    Only if ICP asks for it. Biometrics (fingerprints) apply to people aged 15 and above. We tell you if you need to go to an ICP centre.

    How long does Emirates ID renewal take?

    ICP's standard time is about 5 working days after approval, and the new card is delivered by courier. The medical test and any biometrics appointment can add a few days, so start early.

    How do I check my Emirates ID renewal status?

    On the ICP website (icp.gov.ae), use the card status page with your application number or Emirates ID number. Or send us a WhatsApp message and we check it for you.

    I lost my Emirates ID. What should I do first?

    We usually first get a lost item certificate (police report) through Sharjah Police or Dubai Police. It is not mandatory but recommended. Then we apply for the replacement card with ICP for you.

    Do I need a police report for a damaged Emirates ID?

    No. A police report is only useful when the card is lost or stolen. A damaged card is replaced with ICP directly.

    Can I get a replacement Emirates ID urgently?

    ICP has an urgent service that issues the card within 24 hours for lost or damaged cards. You need to visit an ICP customer happiness centre for it.

    What do I need to replace a lost Emirates ID?

    Usually a passport copy with your UAE visa, your Emirates ID number and a recent photo with a white background. We confirm the list for your case.

    Attestation & translation

    How long does certificate attestation take?

    It depends on the country and the document. UAE MOFA attestation of a document already attested abroad is usually quick, while the full chain from the home country takes longer. We give you the timeline for your document.

    Do I need to send the original document?

    For attestation, yes, the original is usually required. For translation, a clear copy is often enough. We tell you what is needed.

    Can you attest Indian certificates?

    Yes. We handle documents from India and more than 100 other countries.

    Is your translation accepted by UAE courts and ministries?

    We arrange legal translation by licensed legal translators, which is the format UAE authorities require.

    How do I apply for MOFA attestation?

    Online on the MOFA website or the UAE MOFA app, logging in with UAE Pass and paying online. If you don't have UAE Pass, we can apply for you.

    How long does MOFA attestation take?

    MOFA says digital attestation is done within about 2 hours in working hours. Paper documents sent by courier usually take 1 to 3 working days inside the UAE.

    What is MOFA digital attestation?

    For digital documents issued by a UAE government entity with a QR code or reference number, MOFA adds an electronic stamp to the PDF. No paper is sent.

    Does my document need translation before MOFA attestation?

    MOFA accepts documents in Arabic or English. Documents in other languages need a legal translation. We do legal translation in Arabic, English, Malayalam, Hindi, Urdu and Tamil.

    How can I check if a MOFA attestation is genuine?

    Go to verify.mofa.gov.ae and enter the reference number shown on the attestation stamp.

    What are the steps to attest an Indian degree for the UAE?

    Usually state attestation (HRD) in India, then MEA, then the UAE Embassy in India, and finally MOFA attestation in the UAE. We check what your certificate already has and handle the rest.

    Do I need to travel to India for degree attestation?

    No. The steps in India can be arranged while you are in the UAE. We tell you how the original is sent and returned.

    My degree has the UAE Embassy stamp. What is left?

    Only MOFA attestation in the UAE. We can apply for it for you.

    How much does Indian degree attestation cost?

    It depends on the state, the certificate and which steps are already done. Send us a photo and we give you the exact total, with government fees and our charge listed separately.

    Police clearance (PCC)

    How long does a UAE police clearance take?

    Does the certificate need attestation?

    Passport & lost passport

    I lost my passport in the UAE. What should I do first?

    Report it to the police and get a lost item certificate (police report). In Sharjah and Dubai you can usually do this online or in the police app. We can help you apply.

    How do I get a new passport?

    Apply at your country's embassy or consulate in the UAE. For Indian citizens, applications now go through Al Hind. Bring a copy of your old passport and the police report. We can type the form and book the appointment.

    I need to travel home urgently. What can I do?

    Your embassy can issue an Emergency Certificate, a one-way travel document that lets you return to your home country. It usually needs the police report and immigration clearance. We can guide you through it.

    What happens to my UAE visa?

    If you have a UAE residence visa, it stays valid. Once you have the new passport, the visa details must be moved to it (passport data update). We can do this for you.
