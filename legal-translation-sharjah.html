Legal translation in Sharjah: what you need to know

Courts, ministries and many government offices in the UAE need documents in Arabic, translated by a licensed legal translator. Here is when you need it and how we handle it for you.

Ask on WhatsApp View Attestation service

What is a legal translation?

A legal translation is a translation done and stamped by a licensed legal translator. UAE courts and most government offices work in Arabic, so a document in another language usually needs a legal translation into Arabic before they accept it. A normal translation, or one you do yourself, is not accepted for official use.

When you usually need it

  • Court cases, police cases and legal papers in Sharjah or any emirate
  • Certificates for official use: marriage, birth, degree, divorce or death certificates
  • Documents for a ministry or authority that are not in Arabic or English
  • Company papers such as contracts, agreements and powers of attorney

Translation and attestation

Translation does not replace attestation. A certificate from another country is usually attested first, and then translated if the office needs it in Arabic. For MOFA attestation, documents in a language other than Arabic or English need a legal translation. If you are not sure of the order, ask us and we check it for your document.

Languages

We arrange legal translation in more than 100 languages, including Arabic, English, Malayalam, Hindi, Urdu and Tamil.

What to send us

  • A clear scan or photo of every page, including stamps and the back side
  • Where the translation is for (court, ministry, MOFA, visa file) and the language you need
  • Names spelled exactly as in your passport

For translation, a clear copy is often enough. If the office also needs the original, we tell you before we start.

The price depends on the language, the number of pages and the type of document. We tell you the exact cost and time before we start.
