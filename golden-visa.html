Who can get a Golden Visa?

The UAE Golden Visa is a long-term residence visa for categories such as property and business investors, skilled professionals who meet the salary rule, outstanding students and graduates, and specialists such as doctors and scientists.

Each category has its own rules and they are updated from time to time. Send us your details and we check your eligibility before you spend anything.

Common categories

  • Property investors
  • Business owners and investors
  • Skilled professionals meeting the salary rule
  • Outstanding students and graduates
  • Doctors, scientists and specialists

Documents to prepare

  • Passport and current visa
  • Emirates ID
  • Proof for your category (salary certificate, degree, title deed or investment)
  • Passport photo, white background
  • Attested degree certificate where required

Who qualifies for the Golden Visa?

Who can apply?

The government groups applicants into categories. The main ones are:

  • Investors in public investments or businesses
  • Real estate investors who own one or more properties worth at least AED 2 million in total. A bank loan from an approved local bank is allowed, and off-plan units also count
  • Entrepreneurs with an approved business or project
  • Exceptional talents and specialists, such as doctors, scientists, engineers and executives who meet the conditions
  • Outstanding students and graduates from UAE schools and universities
  • Humanitarian pioneers and frontline heroes

Each category has its own conditions, such as salary, experience or grades, and they are updated from time to time. That is why we check your details against the current rules before you pay anything.

What are the benefits?

  • Residence for 5 or 10 years, and it can be renewed
  • No employer sponsor needed
  • You can sponsor family members, including your spouse and children
  • You can stay outside the UAE for longer than the usual six months without losing your residence

Documents to prepare

  • Passport and current visa (if you have one)
  • Emirates ID (if you live in the UAE)
  • Passport photo with a white background
  • Proof for your category, for example a title deed, salary certificate, work contract, degree or grade certificate
  • Attested degree certificate where your category needs one

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a sponsor for the Golden Visa?

No. The Golden Visa is self-sponsored, so you do not need an employer to sponsor your residence.

Can I sponsor my family on a Golden Visa?

Yes. Golden Visa holders can usually sponsor their spouse and children. We can process their visas together with yours.

How do I know which category I fit?

Send us your salary, qualifications or investment details on WhatsApp. We review them and tell you which category, if any, fits your case.

Does my degree need attestation?

For the professional and student categories, degree certificates usually need attestation. We arrange degree attestation too.
