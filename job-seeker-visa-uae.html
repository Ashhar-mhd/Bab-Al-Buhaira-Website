Job seeker visa in the UAE: who qualifies and how to apply

Want to come to the UAE to look for a job without a sponsor? The job exploration visa lets eligible people enter on their own. Here is who qualifies, what is needed, and how we apply for you.

Ask on WhatsApp View UAE visas service

What is the job seeker visa?

ICP calls it the Job Exploration Visa. It lets a person enter the UAE to look for work without a host or sponsor in the UAE. ICP offers it as a 60-day or 90-day visa, both single entry.

Who qualifies?

ICP says the applicant must be one of these:

  • Skilled worker: classified by MOHRE (the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation) in professional skill levels 1, 2 or 3.
  • Recent graduate: from one of the top 500 universities in the world (as listed by the UAE Ministry of Education), with no more than two years since graduation.

Not sure if you qualify? Send us your degree and job title and we check first.

Documents needed

  • Passport valid for at least 6 months
  • Personal photo
  • For skilled workers: MOHRE certificate showing the skill level
  • For graduates: bachelor's degree, and the Ministry of Education certificate that the university is in the top 500
  • Valid health insurance in the UAE

Fees and guarantee

Along with the ICP fees, ICP takes a financial guarantee of AED 1,000. It can be refunded after the visa is cancelled or after you leave the UAE, as long as there are no residency violations. If you stay past the visa, the overstay fine is AED 50 per day.

How to apply

The application is made with ICP, online with UAE Pass or at a service centre. ICP says it usually takes about 2 days. We can prepare and submit it for you, and arrange the health insurance.

Found a job?

When you get a job offer, your employer applies for your work permit and residence visa. We can help with the visa process too.

We tell you the exact total before you pay, with the ICP fees, the refundable guarantee and our service charge listed separately.

Questions

Job seeker visa: common questions

Can I come to the UAE to look for a job without a sponsor?

Yes, if you qualify for the job exploration visa. ICP issues it without the need for a host in the UAE.

Who can get the UAE job seeker visa?

ICP says skilled workers in MOHRE skill levels 1 to 3, or graduates of the world's top 500 universities within two years of graduation.

How long is the UAE job seeker visa?

ICP offers 60-day and 90-day versions, both single entry.

Is there a deposit for the job seeker visa?

Yes. ICP takes a financial guarantee of AED 1,000, which can be refunded after the visa is cancelled or you leave the UAE, if there are no violations.
