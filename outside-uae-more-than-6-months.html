Outside the UAE for more than 6 months?

If you hold a UAE residence visa and stay abroad for more than 6 months, you need ICP's approval to come back. Here is how ICP's permit works, and we can apply for you while you are still abroad.

ICP's permit to stay outside more than 6 months

ICP has a service for residents who stay outside the UAE for more than six months, as long as there is a valid reason. ICP's conditions are:

  • The application is made from outside the UAE.
  • You can apply after 180 days outside the UAE.
  • Your residence must still have more than 30 days of validity when you apply.
  • You must give a valid reason, with proof, for staying outside more than 180 days.
  • If a company sponsors you, the company or you can apply.

After approval: enter within 30 days

Once ICP approves, you must enter the UAE within 30 days, counting the approval date.

Fines and fees

ICP lists a fine of AED 100 for every 30 days (or part of it) spent outside the UAE, plus ICP service fees. We tell you the exact total for your case before you pay.

Who does not need it

  • Golden, Green and Blue residence holders, and the husband of a UAE national woman sponsored by her, can enter the UAE at any time while the residence is valid.
  • Dubai residents: ICP says this service does not apply to residents of Dubai. Dubai visas are handled by GDRFA, so ask us and we check your case.

What to send us

  • Passport copy with your UAE visa, and your Emirates ID
  • Proof of the reason you stayed outside, for example medical or study papers
  • Your sponsor's details, if a company or family member sponsors you

ICP lists the service as taking about 2 days. You do not need to come to the UAE first: we apply while you are abroad.

We tell you the exact total before you pay, with the government fee and our service charge listed separately.

Last checked 14 November 2026 against ICP (icp.gov.ae, Issuance of a Permit to Stay Outside the Country for More Than 6 Months). Government rules can change, so we confirm everything before you pay.

Questions

Outside UAE over 6 months: common questions

I stayed outside the UAE for more than 6 months. Can I come back on my residence visa?

ICP has a permit for residents who stayed outside more than 6 months with a valid reason. You apply from outside the UAE after 180 days, and your residence must still have more than 30 days of validity.

How long do I have to enter the UAE after the permit is approved?

ICP says you must enter the UAE within 30 days, counting the approval date.

Do Golden Visa holders need this permit?

No. ICP says Golden, Green and Blue residence holders can enter the UAE at any time while their residence is valid.

Does this ICP permit apply to Dubai residence visas?

No. ICP says this service does not apply to residents of Dubai. Ask us and we check what applies to your Dubai visa.
