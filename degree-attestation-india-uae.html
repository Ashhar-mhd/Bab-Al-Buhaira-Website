Indian degree certificate attestation for the UAE

Need your Indian degree attested for a UAE job, higher studies or a Golden Visa? The certificate goes through several offices in India and then MOFA in the UAE. Here are the steps, and we handle them for you.

Ask on WhatsApp View Attestation service

When do you need your degree attested?

  • Employment visa: for jobs where the work permit or job title needs a degree.
  • Higher studies or equivalency in the UAE.
  • Golden Visa categories that need a degree.

The usual steps for an Indian degree

  1. State attestation in India: by the education department (HRD) of the state where the university is.
  2. MEA attestation: by the Ministry of External Affairs, India.
  3. UAE Embassy in India: attestation by the UAE Embassy or Consulate.
  4. MOFA in the UAE: the final attestation by the UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

The steps in India can be arranged while you are in the UAE. We tell you how the original reaches India and comes back for your case.

What to send us

  • Clear copy of the degree certificate (front and back)
  • Mark sheets or transcripts, if asked
  • Passport copy
  • Emirates ID and visa copy (if you live in the UAE)

Before you start

  • The certificate must not be laminated.
  • The name must match your passport. If it doesn't, tell us first.
  • If only MOFA attestation in the UAE is missing, we can do that part quickly. See MOFA attestation.

The price depends on the state, the certificate and the steps already done. We tell you the exact total before you pay, with the government fees and our service charge listed separately.

Questions

Indian degree attestation: common questions

What are the steps to attest an Indian degree for the UAE?

Usually state attestation (HRD) in India, then MEA, then the UAE Embassy in India, and finally MOFA attestation in the UAE. We check what your certificate already has and handle the rest.

Do I need to travel to India for degree attestation?

No. The steps in India can be arranged while you are in the UAE. We tell you how the original is sent and returned.

My degree has the UAE Embassy stamp. What is left?

Only MOFA attestation in the UAE. We can apply for it for you.

How much does Indian degree attestation cost?

It depends on the state, the certificate and which steps are already done. Send us a photo and we give you the exact total, with government fees and our charge listed separately.
